## Settled scope

Publish `co_ideam` using direct, unauthenticated `LiveStages` retrieval from the official Open Data Cloud Object Store (`https://datos.ideam.gov.co/s3-estacionesideam/`) operated by Colombia's **Instituto de Hidrología, Meteorología y Estudios Ambientales (`IDEAM`)**, backed by RivRetrieve's shared observation cache.

Selecting IDEAM's official S3 open-data store (`observaciones/historicos/` alongside `catalogo/estaciones/csv/catalogo.csv` and `catalogo/variables/csv/variables.csv`) supersedes the older `aquariuswebportal.ideam.gov.co` JSON grid workflow in legacy `hydrodownloadR` (`CO_IDEAM`). Empirical verification on 2026-11-06 established four facts supporting IDEAM S3 as `co_ideam`'s sole observation upstream:

1. **Historical continuity back to 1934 (`HIS_*` integration):** On `aquariuswebportal.ideam.gov.co`, pre-2016 records migrated from IDEAM's legacy SISDHIM database are split into separate `HIS_*` series alongside post-2016 operational series. In `s3-estacionesideam/observaciones/historicos/`, IDEAM has already concatenated the pre-2016 `HIS_*` archive and post-2016 operational records into one continuous file per station and series label, preserving records starting in 1934 on the Río Magdalena and matching AQUARIUS values to full floating-point precision through the previous calendar day.
2. **Complete station coverage across all 20 series:** Operational daily mean discharge (`CAUDAL.Q_MEDIA_D`) on `aquariuswebportal.ideam.gov.co` is populated at only 171 gauges, omitting more than 750 gauges that IDEAM publishes cleanly in `s3-estacionesideam` (943 total `Q_MEDIA_D` stations, 941 on flowing watercourses). Across all 20 hydrological series, S3 station counts equal or exceed the union of AQUARIUS operational and `HIS_*` datasets, and 99.0 percent of stations with populated manual staff-gauge readings (`NVLM_CON`) on AQUARIUS already publish daily stage (`NV_MEDIA_D`) in S3.
3. **Preservation of active quality-control status (`nivelAprobacion` versus `Grade`):** AQUARIUS WebPortal exports define 22 `Grade` codes alongside 3 `Approval` levels (`900 - Preliminar`, `1100 - En revisión`, `1200 - Definitivo`). Across sampled approved, preliminary, legacy `HIS_*`, hourly, and automatic AQUARIUS exports, IDEAM leaves `Grade` unpopulated as `50 - INDEFINIDO` and records operational validation status in `Approval Level`. IDEAM S3 exports that exact approval status per observation in its `nivelAprobacion` column (`Preliminar`, `En revisión`, `Definitivo`), so omitting AQUARIUS loses no populated observation quality markers.
4. **Operational reliability and timestamp fidelity:** AQUARIUS WebPortal's `/Data/DatasetGrid` JSON endpoint appends a literal `"Z"` suffix to `UTC-05:00` local timestamps (which caused a one-day shift when parsed as UTC and converted to `America/Bogota` in legacy client code), times out after more than 45 seconds on multi-decade hourly series (`alldata=true`), requires authentication (`HTTP 401`) on `/api/v1/export/*`, and advertises roughly 6,000 unpopulated shell datasets (`StartOfRecord = null`). IDEAM S3 serves deterministic UTF-8 CSV objects updated daily without session state or grid timeouts.

In line with RivRetrieve's supported quantities (`discharge`, `stage`, and water `temperature`), include all 20 river discharge and river stage series published in `s3-estacionesideam/observaciones/historicos/`:

1. **Discharge (`discharge`, native `m3/s`, returned `m3/s`):**
   - **Hourly (`Horario`):** `CAUDAL_H` (574 total S3 stations).
   - **Daily (`Diario`):** Daily mean `Q_MEDIA_D` (943), daily maximum `Q_MX_D` (537), and daily minimum `Q_MN_D` (537).
   - **Monthly (`Mensual`):** Monthly mean `Q_MEDIA_M` (962), monthly maximum `Q_MX_M` (964), and monthly minimum `Q_MN_M` (965).
   - **Annual (`Anual`):** Annual mean `Q_MEDIA_A` (961), annual maximum `Q_MX_A` (964), and annual minimum `Q_MN_A` (965).
2. **Stage (`stage`, native `cm`, returned `m`):**
   - **Hourly (`Horario`):** Validated hourly stage `NIVEL_H` (812 total S3 stations).
   - **Daily (`Diario`):** Daily mean `NV_MEDIA_D` (1,388), daily maximum `NV_MX_D` (753), and daily minimum `NV_MN_D` (754).
   - **Monthly (`Mensual`):** Monthly mean `NV_MEDIA_M` (1,436), monthly maximum `NV_MX_M` (1,306), and monthly minimum `NV_MN_M` (1,413).
   - **Annual (`Anual`):** Annual mean `NV_MEDIA_A` (1,421), annual maximum `NV_MX_A` (1,296), and annual minimum `NV_MN_A` (1,412).
3. **Water temperature (`temperature`):** IDEAM publishes no river or surface-water temperature series in `s3-estacionesideam` or `aquariuswebportal.ideam.gov.co` (published temperature variables cover only air temperature at 2 m and soil temperature at 10 to 50 cm).

Include all flowing-watercourse stations published in IDEAM's S3 hydrological datasets (`1,469` total hydrological stations before water-body filtering), covering both stations operated directly by `IDEAM` (`1,320`) and partner stations (`149`) operated by regional environmental authorities (`CAR`, `CVC`, `CORANTIOQUIA`, `CAS`), public utilities (`EPM`, `EMGESA`, `ISAGEN`), and municipal or private networks that IDEAM curates and distributes under the same open-data terms. Do not compute derived aggregates across frequencies or stitch separate series together.

### Documented upstream options excluded from `co_ideam`

- **Uncalibrated raw automatic-sensor feeds (`datos.gov.co` `bdmn-sqnh`, `vfth-yucv`, `pt9a-aamx`, S3 `observaciones/instantaneos/`, and AQUARIUS `NV_AUT_*`):** Excluded because raw automatic logger feeds (`0230`, `0231`, `0232`) emit uncalibrated values prior to gauge-offset and unit normalization, exhibiting tenfold and one-tenth scaling errors relative to validated stage at the same gauge and date, whereas `observaciones/historicos/` already updates daily through the previous day with calibrated `cm` and `m3/s` values.
- **River sediment series (`CM_D`, `CM_M`, `CM_A`, `TR_QS_M`, `TR_QS_A`, deferred):** Available upstream in `observaciones/historicos/` as daily, monthly, and annual suspended sediment concentration (`mg/l` and `Kg/m3`) and monthly and annual suspended sediment transport (`Ktn/dia`). Deferred in line with RivRetrieve's current `discharge`, `stage`, and water `temperature` scope while documented in the research record and user documentation.
- **Single-station AQUARIUS technician backup labels and airport cloud-base height (`ALTURA`):** Exclude the ten single-station working or backup series on AQUARIUS WebPortal (`CAUDAL_H_ORIG`, `Q_MEDIA_D_ORIG`, `Q_MX_D_ORIG`, `Q_MN_D_ORIG`, `NIVEL_H_ORIG`, `NV_MEDIA_D_ORIG`, `NV_MX_D_ORIG`, and `NV_MN_D_ORIG` at gauge `44117010`; `BALANCE_SUMAPAZ` at `21197010`; and `Caudal - Q` at `26157170`) and exclude `ALTURA.NBAL1_CON` through `NBAL4_CON` (meteorological cloud-base ceiling height at 29 airport stations).
- **Standing-water bodies, marine gauges, and synthetic test entries:** Restrict `co_ideam` to flowing watercourses (rivers, streams, *quebradas*, *caños*, and canals). Exclude synthetic placeholder station `0010000000` (`ESTACION FICTICIA`), marine tide gauges (`Categoria == "Meteorológica Marina"` or `Corriente` on the Caribbean Sea or Pacific Ocean), and standing-water bodies (`EMBALSE`, `LAGO`, `LAGUNA`, `CIÉNAGA`), while retaining river gauges whose station or municipality name contains `Laguna`.

## Access conditions and operational boundaries

- **Anonymous global HTTPS access:** All selected `https://datos.ideam.gov.co/s3-estacionesideam/*` routes respond over standard anonymous HTTPS `GET` without credentials, cookies, tokens, or datacenter IP restrictions.
- **Deduplicated station and variable catalogues:** Use `catalogo/estaciones/csv/catalogo.csv` (`9,930` unique station codes matching 100 percent of S3 hydrological gauges in `EPSG:4326`) and `catalogo/variables/csv/variables.csv` alongside paginated S3 `ListObjectsV2` XML listings (`?list-type=2&prefix=observaciones/historicos/csv/{LABEL}/&max-keys=1000`) rather than Socrata `hp9r-jxuu`, which contains duplicate rows and swapped GeoJSON coordinate order.
- **Deterministic CSV retrieval and missing-series HTTP 404 behavior:** Acquire per-station CSV files directly at `observaciones/historicos/csv/{LABEL}/{station_id_10digit}-{LABEL}.csv` (`fechaObservacion,valorObservado,nivelAprobacion`). Companion Hive-partitioned Parquet files carry identical rows and float64 values but require an extra `ListObjectsV2` request per series to discover random Spark part filenames. Requesting an unpublished `(station_id, LABEL)` CSV returns `HTTP 404` with an S3 `NoSuchKey` XML body, cleanly distinguishing absent series from transport failures. Maintain polite client request pacing (at most 1 request per second).

## Terms and attribution

Official Colombian Open Data metadata cards published by *Oficina de Informática - IDEAM* on `datos.gov.co` (`hp9r-jxuu`, `jxnq-r3i9`, `79gx-f3v5`, `9fxi-fthb`, `ig79-2jr9`, and `57sv-p2fu`, *"Datos de Estaciones de IDEAM y de Terceros"*) and IDEAM's institutional Open Data page (`https://www.ideam.gov.co/transparencia/datos-abiertos/seccion-de-datos-abiertos`) license both the national station catalogue and the `s3-estacionesideam` hydrological series under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (`CC BY-SA 4.0`)** under Colombia's Transparency and Open Data Law (*Ley 1712 de 2014*). The `"Términos y condiciones"` link in the `ideam.gov.co` footer points to IDEAM's *Ley 1581 de 2012* personal-data privacy policy and adds no restriction on hydrometeorological open data.

Follow RivRetrieve's established `lt_lhmt` (`CC BY-SA 4.0`) model:
- RivRetrieve's Python library code remains licensed under MIT.
- Hydrological observations are fetched live on demand from IDEAM S3 (`LiveStages`) and are never redistributed in the package.
- The narrow packaged `co_ideam` `station_metadata` projection carries explicit attribution to `IDEAM` and the `CC BY-SA 4.0` ShareAlike notice in `STATION_METADATA_NOTICE`, `croissant.json`, `docs/station-metadata.md`, and `docs/providers/co_ideam.md`.
