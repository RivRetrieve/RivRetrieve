Establish source-backed series identities, physical and temporal fact definitions, clipping semantics, and observation CSV invariants for Colombia's national hydrometeorological provider, `co_ideam`, under [Effort #723](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/723) and [Program #460](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/460). Target `port/co-ideam` and keep `main` unchanged until [final review #728](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/728). Follow [provider-port step 3](../../docs/maintenance/provider-ports.md), the [evidence process](../../docs/maintenance/evidence.md), and the established [source-and-access](2026-11-06-co-ideam-source-scope-and-access.md) and [station-catalogue](2026-11-06-co-ideam-station-catalogue-and-metadata.md) contracts.

## Distinct published series identities (`ideam.s3.historicos`)

IDEAM's Open Data Cloud Object Store (`https://datos.ideam.gov.co/s3-estacionesideam/`) publishes 20 hydrological observation prefixes under `observaciones/historicos/csv/{etiqueta}/{Codigo}-{etiqueta}.csv` (`10` river discharge series and `10` river water-level stage series across `1,372` canonical flowing-watercourse stations and `19,690` station-series pairs):

- **Discharge (`10` series):** `CAUDAL_H` (`caudal_h`), `Q_MEDIA_D` (`q_media_d`), `Q_MX_D` (`q_mx_d`), `Q_MN_D` (`q_mn_d`), `Q_MEDIA_M` (`q_media_m`), `Q_MX_M` (`q_mx_m`), `Q_MN_M` (`q_mn_m`), `Q_MEDIA_A` (`q_media_a`), `Q_MX_A` (`q_mx_a`), and `Q_MN_A` (`q_mn_a`).
- **Stage (`10` series):** `NIVEL_H` (`nivel_h`), `NV_MEDIA_D` (`nv_media_d`), `NV_MX_D` (`nv_mx_d`), `NV_MN_D` (`nv_mn_d`), `NV_MEDIA_M` (`nv_media_m`), `NV_MX_M` (`nv_mx_m`), `NV_MN_M` (`nv_mn_m`), `NV_MEDIA_A` (`nv_media_a`), `NV_MX_A` (`nv_mx_a`), and `NV_MN_A` (`nv_mn_a`).

Every station-product file is a distinct upstream publication keyed by `SourceIdentity(namespace="ideam.s3.historicos", published_id=etiqueta)`. Never collapse, substitute, or derive daily, monthly, or yearly series from sub-daily or finer-resolution series (`GCI-VH-MO001` v03 and cross-series verification confirm that post-2016 daily means use piecewise-linear trapezoidal integration in AQUARIUS and daily extrema include sub-hourly logger peaks and extraordinary flood crest readings absent from `NIVEL_H` / `CAUDAL_H`).

## Quantities, native units, zero/negative observations, and `gauge_zero` stage reference

- **Quantities and units (`variables.csv` and all 20 `datos.gov.co` Socrata dataset cards):**
  - All 10 `CAUDAL_H` / `Q_*` series establish `quantity="discharge"`, `source_unit="m3/s"`, `normalized_unit="m3/s"`, canonical target `"m3/s"` (exact factor `1.0`).
  - All 10 `NIVEL_H` / `NV_*` series establish `quantity="stage"`, `source_unit="cm"`, `normalized_unit="cm"`, canonical target `"m"` (exact factor `0.01`).
- **Stage vertical reference (`vertical_reference="gauge_zero"`, `vertical_datum=None`):**
  - Official IDEAM hydrological methodology manual `GCI-VH-MO001` (v03, July 2025, pp. 14, 23-29, 56-57, 71-73) and validation manual `GCI-VH-M028` (v03, July 2025, pp. 38, 41) define `NIVEL` in `cm` as water surface height relative to the local staff-gauge or limnigraph zero (*cero de la mira / limnímetro*). High-Andean river gauges in S3 (such as `0026157160` `CONEJERAS1 - AUT` at `Altitud = 4,685 m`, reporting `1.0` to `8.0 cm`) confirm empirically that S3 publishes local relative stage in `cm` rather than sea-level elevation (*cota*).
  - Establish `vertical_reference="gauge_zero"` on all 10 stage series while keeping `vertical_datum` `not_established` (`None`), because S3 publishes neither a gauge's `Cota Cero` tie (*real* m.s.n.m. Buenaventura versus *arbitraria*) nor historical `Cota Cero` shift epochs (`GCI-VH-M028`, p. 38).
- **Zero flows and negative river stage:** Preserve genuine `0.0 m3/s` discharge observations (witnessed on intermittent watercourses such as `0016057010` `EL ANIME`) and `0.0 cm` or negative river stage readings below local gauge zero (`GCI-VH-MO001`, p. 29 notes that $H_0$ in $Q = a(H - H_0)^b$ can be positive or negative relative to staff-gauge zero).

## Frequencies, statistics, and unestablished `temporal_support`

- **Sub-daily `CAUDAL_H` and `NIVEL_H` (`2` series):** Keep `frequency=None`, `statistic=None`, and `temporal_support=None` (`not_established`). In `variables.csv`, `periodo="Horario"` appears alongside blank `tipo_estadistica=""`, while Socrata cards `79gx-f3v5` and `ig79-2jr9`, `GCI-VH-MO001` (`Tabla 10` p. 58, pp. 66, 82), and retained S3 CSVs confirm that both prefixes mix hourly (`1 h`) automatic/limnigraphic telemetry (e.g. `0026177030`) with conventional twice-daily (`06:00:00` and `18:00:00`) staff-gauge readings (e.g. `0011017010` and `0011107010`). IDEAM also leaves undocumented whether sub-hourly (`2` to `15 min`) logger measurements are sampled on the hour or averaged into `NIVEL_H`.
- **Daily, monthly, and yearly means (`Q_MEDIA_D/M/A` and `NV_MEDIA_D/M/A`, `6` series):** Establish `frequency="daily"` / `"monthly"` / `"yearly"` and `statistic="mean"` from `variables.csv` (`periodo` in `{"Diario", "Mensual", "Anual"}`, `tipo_estadistica="Promedio"`), all corresponding `datos.gov.co` Socrata cards, and `GCI-VH-MO001` (pp. 44-47, 50-51, 79-86), while keeping `temporal_support=None` (`not_established`). Pre-2016 `SISDHIM` archives computed arithmetic means of discrete daily readings ($H_d = \frac{1}{n}\sum H_i$, `GCI-VH-M028` Eq. 2, p. 39), whereas post-2016 AQUARIUS (`8 - Succeeding Avg.`) integrates piecewise-linearly across `[00:00, 24:00]` (for twice-daily `06:00`/`18:00` gauges, $\frac{1}{16}H_{\text{prev},18} + \frac{7}{16}H_{06} + \frac{7}{16}H_{18} + \frac{1}{16}H_{\text{next},06}$), and monthly/annual means include partial commissioning or gapped periods (`2` to `16` days in a month).
- **Daily, monthly, and yearly extrema (`Q_MX_D/M/A`, `Q_MN_D/M/A`, `NV_MX_D/M/A`, `NV_MN_D/M/A`, `12` series):** Establish `frequency="daily"` / `"monthly"` / `"yearly"` and `statistic="max"` (`*_MX_*`, `tipo_estadistica="Maxima"`) or `statistic="min"` (`*_MN_*`, `tipo_estadistica="Minima"`), while keeping `temporal_support=None` (`not_established`). Cross-series checks confirm `NV_MX_D >= max(NIVEL_H)` without exception (strictly exceeding `max(NIVEL_H)` on days with sub-hourly logger peaks or extraordinary flood crest readings), `NV_MX_M == max(NV_MX_D)` in the post-2016 AQUARIUS era, and `NV_MX_M > max(NV_MEDIA_D)` across ~99% of pre-2016 `SISDHIM` station-months (resolving loose *"Máximo Medio Mensual"* prose on p. 48 of `GCI-VH-MO001`), while partial months and years prevent asserting complete calendar-window `temporal_support`.

## Time zone, Colombia's 1992-1993 *Hora Gaviria*, and clipping axes

Zero S3 CSVs, `variables.csv` rows, or `datos.gov.co` Socrata cards state an explicit time zone or UTC offset (`GCI-VH-M028` p. 34 and `GCI-VH-MO001` p. 82 note `06:00 y 18:00 HLC` for conventional observers, while AQUARIUS WebPortal metadata displays `UTC-05:00`). Empirical inspection across Colombia's 1992-1993 emergency Daylight Saving Time (*Hora Gaviria*, `1992-05-03` to `1993-02-06`, when civil clocks advanced from `UTC-05:00` to `UTC-04:00`) proves that IDEAM's S3 Spark exporter formatted fixed `UTC-05:00` AQUARIUS timestamps through an `America/Bogota` tzdb formatter without emitting UTC offsets:

- Outside `1992-05-03..1993-02-06`, `100%` of daily, monthly (`YYYY-MM-01`), and yearly (`YYYY-01-01`) aggregate rows carry `00:00:00`; inside the *Hora Gaviria* window, daily (`Q_MEDIA_D`, `NV_MEDIA_D`), monthly (`1992-06-01` through `1993-02-01`), and yearly (`1993-01-01`) aggregate rows carry `01:00:00`.
- Conventional `CAUDAL_H` and `NIVEL_H` readings shift from `06:00:00` / `18:00:00` to `07:00:00` / `19:00:00` during *Hora Gaviria*, and on the fallback night **`1993-02-06 23:00:00`** S3 CSVs emit **duplicate local wall-clock timestamps** for two distinct physical hours (`0011107010-NIVEL_H.csv` records `415.0 cm` and `414.0 cm` both stamped `1993-02-06 23:00:00`; `0035077140-CAUDAL_H.csv` and `0011037020-NIVEL_H.csv` likewise repeat `1993-02-06 23:00:00`). Tagging series with `time_zone="America/Bogota"` would cause `rr.to_utc()` (`fold=0`) to project both fallback observations onto the same UTC instant, whereas tagging `time_zone="UTC-05:00"` would misinterpret the `01:00:00` / `07:00:00` / `19:00:00` *Hora Gaviria* wall-clock strings by one hour.

Accordingly:

- Keep `time_zone=None`, `day_definition=None`, `timestamp_anchor=None`, and `label_time=None` across all 20 series (pairing with `ZoneValue("unknown")` in Step 4).
- Declare `ClippingAxis.CALENDAR_DATE` on the **6 daily series** (`Q_MEDIA_D`, `Q_MX_D`, `Q_MN_D`, `NV_MEDIA_D`, `NV_MX_D`, `NV_MN_D`) so `1992-1993` daily rows stamped `YYYY-MM-DD 01:00:00` clip cleanly by calendar date, and `ClippingAxis.SOURCE_TIMESTAMP` on the **14 sub-daily, monthly, and yearly series**, preserving folded-hour duplicate rows verbatim without deduplication.

## CSV schema, missing-value encoding, and `nivelAprobacion` source quality labels

- **Uniform 3-column CSV contract:** Every S3 hydrological CSV begins with the exact UTF-8 header `fechaObservacion,valorObservado,nivelAprobacion`, followed by non-decreasing `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` timestamps and finite decimal `valorObservado` strings (`0` blank value cells and `0` numeric sentinel codes such as `-999` or `-9999`; missing observations are omitted rows). Note for provenance transparency that `GCI-VH-MO001` (pp. 114-116) documents historical `SISDHIM` gap-filling via the normal-ratio method (*Método de la razón de valores normales*) using correlated gauges without a separate imputation flag in S3 CSVs.
- **Original source quality/approval labels (`nivelAprobacion`):** Every S3 observation row carries IDEAM's approval-stage column `nivelAprobacion`, exhibiting four witnessed values in S3:
  - `"Preliminar"` (corresponding to AQUARIUS approval level `900 - Preliminar`),
  - `"En revisión"` (corresponding to AQUARIUS approval level `1100 - En revisión`),
  - `"Definitivo"` (corresponding to AQUARIUS approval level `1200 - Definitivo`), and
  - `""` (empty string on legacy migrated monthly records, witnessed across all `591` rows of `0011037020-Q_MX_M.csv`, `1971-2021`).
  AQUARIUS WebPortal's separate `Grade` attribute remains unpopulated (`50 - INDEFINIDO`) on IDEAM exports. Following RivRetrieve's cross-library contract, preserve all observation rows regardless of `nivelAprobacion` without filtering or ranking, and record `APPROVAL_VOCABULARY` in `co_ideam/series.py` (and in `docs/providers/co_ideam.md` during Step 7) so downstream maintainers have complete documentation of IDEAM's original per-observation quality/approval labels if RivRetrieve exposes source quality columns in the future.
