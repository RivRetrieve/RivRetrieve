# Austria eHYD series identity and physical meaning

Deliver evidence-backed, tested source-series definitions for Austria's federal hydrographic provider, `at_ehyd`, under [Effort #614](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/614) and [Program #525](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/525). Users and later retrieval code must distinguish published eHYD records across validated and telemetry routes and interpret established physical facts without inheriting unsupported meanings.

Completing the research, definitions, aligned catalogue metadata, and verification described here completes #614; publishing this vision alone does not complete the Effort. Work targets `port/at-ehyd` and leaves `main` unchanged until [final review #619](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/619). Keep `at_ehyd` registered as `CatalogueOnly()` during #614; observation acquisition, gap/window parsing, and live/bulk integration belong to [#615](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/615) and [#616](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/616).

## Preserve published distinctions

Build on the [offline station catalogue](2026-10-31-at-ehyd-offline-station-catalogue.md) across all `746` canonical river gauges (`739` `owf` river gauges and `7` telemetry-only `Pegel Aktuell` river gauges). Keep yearbook-validated `Geprüfte Daten` (`owf`, `7` products) and unvalidated `Pegel Aktuell` telemetry (`pegelBgis` `2` products, `pegelLongterm` `2` products) distinct even at overlapping stations:

- **`owf` validated CSV products (`ehyd.owf`):** Keep all `7` in-scope river series distinct (`owf_abfl_tagesmittel`, `owf_abfl_monatsminima`, `owf_abfl_monatsmaxima`, `owf_wasserst_tagesmittel`, `owf_wasserst_monatsminima`, `owf_wasserst_monatsmaxima`, and `owf_wassertemp_monatsmittel`). Preserve the `4,275` witnessed station-product associations, including `WT-Monatsmittel-208058.csv` and `WT-Monatsmittel-213041.csv` (which declare valid `Einheit: [°C]` and `Exportzeitreihe` headers with empty `Invalid; Lücke` bodies), while keeping the `3` `Einheit: []` stage files (`203539`, `204388`, `211078`) at `"unknown"` availability.
- **`pegelBgis` sub-daily telemetry (`ehyd.pegel_bgis`):** Map the `categories` timestamp array and `data` value array for `Abfluss` (`pegel_bgis_abfluss`, `266` gauges including empty-array `209288`) and `Wasserstand` (`pegel_bgis_wasserstand`, `14` gauges); keep `0`-byte outage captures (`201087`, `212167`) at `"unknown"` availability. Static warning thresholds (`niedrigwasser`, `mittelwasser`, `erhmittelwasser`, `hochwasser1`, `hochwasser2`, `hochwasser3`) and scalar header snapshot fields (`wert`, `zp`) are station/header metadata, not observation series.
- **`pegelLongterm` daily comparison JSON (`ehyd.pegel_longterm`, `published_id="current_value"`):** Map only `current_value` on the `points_in_time` calendar-date grid for `Abfluss` (`pegel_longterm_abfluss`, `268` gauges including all-null `212167`) and `Wasserstand` (`pegel_longterm_wasserstand`, `14` gauges). Exclude the `5` long-term calendar-day climatological summary envelopes (`min_data` with `min_year`, `max_data` with `max_year`, `mit_data`, `p5_data`, and `p95_data` computed over `data_from` to `data_till`), and exclude `last_year_value` because the JSON plots prior-year values onto the current year's `365`-day `points_in_time` vector without an independent date axis (making leap/non-leap year transitions ambiguous and matching its omission from eHYD's own chart CSV export).

## Physical quantities, units, negative values, and vertical reference

- **Quantities and dimensional admission:** Map `Abfluss` in `m³/s` (`normalized_unit="m3/s"`, target `m3/s`, factor `1.0`), `Wasserstand` in `cm` (`normalized_unit="cm"`, target `m`, factor `0.01`), and `WTemperatur` in `°C` (`normalized_unit="degC"`, target `degC`, factor `1.0`), handling unbracketed `Einheit: m³/s` (`204032`) and nested `Einheit: [ [°C]]` (`200550`, `200568`).
- **Dated gauge-zero (`Pegelnullpunkt`) reference without per-series datum collapse:** Official `Pegelordnung 2014` (Section 1(4) and Section 2.3, referencing ÖNORM B 2400) defines river `Wasserstand` (`W`) in `cm` relative to the station's local `Pegelnullpunkt`, establishing `vertical_reference="gauge_zero"` across `owf` and `Pegel Aktuell` stage series. Across the `739` `owf` river gauges, `543` (`73.5%`, `537` with multiple distinct heights) list `2` to `12` dated `Pegelnullpunkt` (`Höhe [m ü.A.]`) validity epochs in `Stammdaten` and `W` CSV preambles (`195` list `1` epoch and `204388` lists `0` epochs) without binding which historical `W` rows were recorded or back-adjusted against which epoch; leave per-series `vertical_datum` `not_established`.
- **Negative values and zero flow:** Preserve genuine negative river stage values below gauge zero (`20` `W-Tagesmittel` and `22` `W-Monatsminima` river gauges down to `-227 cm` / `-2.27 m`; `8` `W-Monatsmaxima` gauges down to `-176 cm`) and negative `WT-Monatsmittel` water temperatures (`29` river gauges down to `-2.8 °C`), as well as legitimate `0,00` zero discharge.

## Temporal meaning, descriptors, gap encodings, and real source exceptions

Adopt only facts established by retained publisher headers, frontend code, and `Pegelordnung 2014`, keeping unresolved facts `not_established` so incompatible `PhysicalPredicate` filters reject rather than guess:

- **`owf` interval mean series (`Q-Tagesmittel`, `W-Tagesmittel`, `WT-Monatsmittel`):** Every one of the `1,610` valid files declares `I,Mit,T,1` (`daily`) or `I,Mit,M,1` (`monthly`) in `Exportzeitreihe` (`204032` `Werfen` uses unparenthesized `Abfluss,I,Mit,T,1,F,Z,50,,,`) and carries the explicit interval rule `Der Intervallwert gilt bis zum nächsten Zeitpunkt mit einem Wert oder Lücke`. Establish `frequency="daily"` / `"monthly"`, `statistic="mean"` (`Exportzeitreihe` `Mit` and `Pegelordnung 2014` Section 9.2.1 / 9.2.2(3)), and `timestamp_anchor="start"` with `ClippingAxis.SOURCE_TIMESTAMP`. Document `Pegelordnung 2014` Section 9.2.1(1), under which historical non-recording staff gauges (*Lattenpegel*, e.g. `Pegellatte-händische Messung` epochs in `Stammdaten`) treat a scheduled observer reading (*Terminbeobachtung*) as the daily value (`Tageswert`). Leave `temporal_support` `not_established` because genuine interval files contain partial commissioning intervals, `01:00:00`-anchored daily intervals (`203265` `Schwaighofbrücke` throughout leap year `1976`), and `21st`-of-month anchored monthly intervals (`212704` `Neudorf`, `21.05.2007` to `21.12.2020`). All `1,610` interval files end with a trailing closing-boundary `Lücke` row (`t_end; Lücke`) terminating the final valid interval rather than encoding a missing observation at `t_end`, while internal `Lücke` rows terminate the preceding interval and mark `[t_gap, t_next)` as missing.
- **`owf` monthly instantaneous extrema (`Q/W-Monatsminima`, `Q/W-Monatsmaxima`) and the `207340` exception:** `Pegelordnung 2014` Section 9.2.2(2) and `Exportzeitreihe` (`M,Min,M,1` / `M,Max,M,1`, plus `M,MIN,M,0` / `M,MAX,M,0` with genuine monthly extrema on `204388` and `211078`) define these series as instantaneous (`Momentanwert`) monthly minima and maxima stamped with exact occurrence timestamps (`DD.MM.YYYY HH:MM:SS`), with `01.MM.YYYY 00:00:00; Lücke` marking missing months. Establish `frequency="monthly"`, `statistic="min"` / `"max"`, and `temporal_support="calendar_month"` (aggregate window of the instantaneous extreme; `timestamp_anchor` remains `not_established` because timestamps record event occurrence rather than window boundaries) for all standard files. On Danube gauge `207340` (`Wilhering`), `W-Monatsminima-207340.csv` declares `Exportzeitreihe: (10001027,Wasserstand,M,Min,T,1,F,Z,0,,,)` and contains `17,532` daily minimum rows (`1976` to `2023`): mirroring `ch_foen` station `7008`, retain `207340`'s witnessed `owf_wasserst_monatsminima` identity, `[cm]`/`gauge_zero` facts, and `statistic="min"`, while leaving `frequency` and `temporal_support` `not_established` on `207340` (and keeping product-table `frequency` `"unknown"` on `owf_wasserst_monatsminima`).
- **`owf` time zone and missing-value token:** Zero `owf` CSV headers state a time zone, and `Pegelordnung 2014` Section 6.2(4) recommends `MEZ` (*"empfiehlt es sich"*) while noting `MEZ` versus `SOZ/MEZ` recording practices; keep `time_zone` and `day_definition` `not_established` across all `owf` series. Across all ~`25M` rows of the `4,275` valid `owf` CSVs, `'Lücke'` is the sole missing-value token (`0` numeric sentinels such as `-999`).
- **`Pegel Aktuell` temporal facts, gap encoding, and source quality vocabulary:** For `pegelBgis`, establish `time_zone="UTC+01:00"` (`MEZ`) from `ehyd-diagram-bundle.body` (`"Zeitangaben in MEZ"`) corroborated across all `279` active river captures spanning the `2026-10-25` Europe/Vienna DST fallback (`0` duplicate timestamps), leaving `frequency`, `statistic`, `temporal_support`, `day_definition`, and `timestamp_anchor` `not_established` across irregular `5 s` to `60 min` steps. For `pegelLongterm`, establish `frequency="daily"` (`ClippingAxis.CALENDAR_DATE`) from `points_in_time` (`YYYY-MM-DD`), leaving `statistic`, `temporal_support`, `day_definition`, `timestamp_anchor`, and `time_zone` `not_established`. Both JSON routes encode missing/future points solely as JSON `null`. Record publisher quality classifications (`Geprüfte Daten` / `Exportzeitreihe` quality field vs `Pegel Aktuell` `"ungeprüfte Rohdaten!"` in `eHYD_Aktuelle_Daten.pdf` p. 16) as source vocabulary without inventing a cross-route ranking.
