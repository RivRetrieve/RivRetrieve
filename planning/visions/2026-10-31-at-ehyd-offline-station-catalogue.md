# Austria eHYD offline station catalogue

Deliver reproducible offline station discovery and source-backed metadata for Austria's federal hydrographic provider, `at_ehyd`, under [Effort #613](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/613) and [Program #525](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/525). Verified completion of the offline catalogue completes #613; publishing this vision alone does not complete #613 or authorize observation retrieval.

Target `port/at-ehyd` and keep `main` unchanged until [final review #619](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/619). Follow [provider-port step 2](../../docs/maintenance/provider-ports.md), the [evidence process](../../docs/maintenance/evidence.md), and the established [source-and-access scope](2026-10-31-at-ehyd-source-scope-and-access.md). Register `at_ehyd` with `CatalogueOnly()` in Step 2, matching the Swiss (`ch_foen`) and Peruvian (`pe_senamhi`) offline-catalogue delivery precedents.

## Discovery outcome and river population

Users can discover established Austrian river gauges offline and inspect source-backed names, water-body names, catchment areas, coordinates, license/attribution metadata, and recognized series candidates without network calls. Build exclusively from explicitly selected retained originals through the shared catalogue schemas, origin gate, metadata projection, and provenance machinery.

The 2026-10-31 Step-2 source audit (`at_ehyd-catalogue-2026-10-31-v1`) reconciled all `798` surface-water (`owf`) stations in `i000501:messstellen_owf`, `Messstellen/json?filter=owf`, and the official national archive `ehyd_messstellen_all_owf.zip` against all `300` live telemetry stations in `i000501:pegel_aktuell` and `PegelAktuell/json`:

- **River-versus-lake (`Seepegel`) boundary:** `owf` (`Sachgebiet: OWF`) combines rivers with standing lakes. All `628` `owf` stations publishing discharge (`owf_abfl_*`) monitor flowing watercourses or gauged lake outflows (such as `214445` `Lunz am See (Wassercluster Summenpegel)` on `Lunzer See mit Seeausleitung`). Among the `170` non-discharge `owf` stations, exactly `59` are standing-water lake or dammed oxbow gauges (`57` carrying `Seefläche` in `Stammdaten` and/or `(Seepegel)` in station name, plus `205575` on `Hinterer Gosausee` and `207217` on `Alte Donau`), while all remaining `111` non-discharge `owf` gauges monitor rivers, streams (including watercourses named `Obernberger Seebach` or `Seeache`), or canals. In `Pegel Aktuell`, `18` of the `300` gauges are standing-lake gauges (`4` reporting lake elevation in `m ü.A` on `Bodensee` and `Neusiedler See`, plus `14` reporting lake stage in `cm` on `Zeller See`, `Wallersee`, `Mattsee`, `Obertrumer See`, `Wolfgangsee`, `Fuschlsee`, `Grabensee`, `Millstätter See`, `Weißensee`, `Pressegger See`, `Faaker See`, `Wörthersee`, `Ossiacher See`, and `Längsee`; all `18` overlap the `59` excluded `owf` lakes).
- **Retained river registry (`746` stations):** Retain all `739` `owf` river stations (`798 - 59`, including `6` decommissioned stations with `aufgelassen` dates and `2` river gauges `207480` `Hainburg Straßenbrücke` on `Donau` and `230102` `Oberaudorf` on `Inn` that publish only deferred sediment series in `owf` and therefore carry `unknown` availability on in-scope products) plus the `7` telemetry-only river gauges present in `Pegel Aktuell` (`282` telemetered river gauges = `275` overlapping `owf` + `7` telemetry-only: `200675`, `202614`, `204180`, `213595`, `215046`, `217240`, `231795`).

## Identity, coordinates, and station metadata

- **Primary identity:** Use the 6-digit `HZBNR` string (`hzbnr`) across `owf` and `Pegel Aktuell`.
- **Coordinates and documented CRS (`EPSG:4326`):** LFRZ OGC API Features (`i000501` collections metadata) explicitly declares `storageCrs: http://www.opengis.net/def/crs/OGC/1.3/CRS84` (`EPSG:4326`) on both `i000501:messstellen_owf` and `i000501:pegel_aktuell`. Across all `798` `owf` stations, `i000501:messstellen_owf` coordinates agree with `EPSG:31287` (`MGI / Austria Lambert`) coordinates in `messstellen_owf.csv` to within `5e-9` degrees (`< 1 mm`), whereas `i000501:pegel_aktuell` telemetry-sensor points differ by up to `2.6e-4` degrees (`~25 m`) on overlapping gauges. Use `i000501:messstellen_owf` geometry for all `739` `owf` river gauges and `i000501:pegel_aktuell` geometry for the `7` telemetry-only river gauges, keeping both source coordinate pairs distinct in the derived native table.
- **Endpoint-scoped station metadata:** Expose `station_name` (`name` in scope `messstellen_owf`; `messstelle` in scope `pegel_aktuell`), `water_body_name` (`gewaesser` in scope `messstellen_owf`; `gewasser` in scope `pegel_aktuell`), and `drainage_area` (`orogr.Einzugsgebiet [km²]` in scope `owf_stammdaten`, unit `km²`, preserving exact German decimal-comma strings such as `"4654,41"` and `"0"` on canals/distributaries). Project fields only for stations present in each source endpoint so the `7` telemetry-only stations (whose `Messstellen/info` returns `[[]]`) report `no_metadata` for `drainage_area` and never acquire union-padded `source_null` `owf` rows, and the `464` non-telemetered `owf` stations never acquire union-padded `pegel_aktuell` rows.
- **Gauge-zero height (`Pegelnullpunkt`) left unprojected in Step 2:** Official `30b Pegelordnung-2014.pdf` (§ 2.3) defines `Pegelnullpunkt` (`Höhe [m ü.A.]` in `Stammdaten-{id}.csv`) as gauge-zero height in metres above Adriatic rather than ground elevation, and `Stammdaten-{id}.csv` publishes it as a multi-row time-versioned validity table (`gültig seit`, `Höhe [m ü.A.]`, with `126` `owf` stations recording multiple historical validity rows and `204388` `Bruck-Neuwiesen` recording an empty table). Meanwhile `mpua04` in `messstellen_owf.csv` carries no standalone unit or datum declaration and lags `2024`/`2025` `Stammdaten` resurveys on `24` stations. Following the `fr_hydroportail` precedent, leave station `elevation` as `no_metadata` rather than collapsing a temporal gauge-zero history into a timeless scalar or transferring `m ü.A.` onto undocumented `mpua04`, and hand off time-versioned `Pegelnullpunkt` handling to [#614](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/614).
- **Terms, attribution, and disclosure:** Bind INSPIRE ISO 19139 records `55e494dd-e0af-4806-95d6-86e30dde0504` (`owf`) and `6a67faa7-3ad7-4faf-91e9-17a518d10685` (`Pegel Aktuell`) establishing `CC BY 4.0` licensing and mandatory attribution `Datenquelle: ehyd.gv.at` in `provider.json` and `STATION_METADATA_NOTICE`, while documenting the live website download notice and 2022 `eHYD_Allgemein.pdf` clause. Complete maintainer disclosure review on the generated 15-file runtime catalogue before merging into `port/at-ehyd`; keep raw observations and restricted receipts private.

## Product associations and witnessed source anomalies

Keep validated `Geprüfte Daten` (`owf`) and unvalidated `Pegel Aktuell` (`pegelBgis`, `pegelLongterm`) separate across 11 river products (`7` `owf` + `4` `Pegel Aktuell`). Preserve source identities and established physical quantities/units (`discharge` in `m³/s` normalized to `m3/s`, `stage` in `cm` normalized to `m`, water `temperature` in `°C` normalized to `degC`) while leaving product-level `frequency`, `statistic`, `period_type`, `period_anchor`, and `published_record_start_date`/`end_date` unestablished (`"unknown"` / `None`) in Step 2:

- **National `owf` archive verification:** All `7` per-station `MessstellenExtraData/owf?id={hzbnr}&file={filenr}` equivalence probes match `ehyd_messstellen_all_owf.zip` byte-for-byte, certifying every `owf` river station's `Stammdaten` and CSV preambles without thousands of individual HTTP requests. Retain deterministic per-station `filenr` routing from `Messstellen/json?filter=owf` (`fjson`).
- **Empty and anomalous `owf` CSV headers:** Across the `739` `owf` river stations, `3` stage CSVs (`W-Monatsminima-203539.csv`, `W-Tagesmittel-204388.csv`, and `W-Tagesmittel-211078.csv`) publish `Einheit: []` (`Invalid ZR` / empty body) and establish neither unit nor recognized series (`unknown` availability, leaving `703` `owf_wasserst_tagesmittel`, `704` `owf_wasserst_monatsminima`, and `705` `owf_wasserst_monatsmaxima` associations, alongside `628` on each of the three `owf_abfl_*` products). By contrast, `WT-Monatsmittel-208058.csv` and `WT-Monatsmittel-213041.csv` publish explicit `Einheit: [°C]` and `Exportzeitreihe` headers despite empty `Invalid; Lücke` bodies (`279` `owf_wassertemp_monatsmittel` river associations). Parsers must also handle unbracketed `Einheit: m³/s` on `204032` and nested `Einheit: [ [°C]]` on `200550` and `200568`.
- **Why temporal meanings and `fjson` year bounds stay unestablished in Step 2:** On Danube gauge `207340` (`Wilhering`), `W-Monatsminima-207340.csv` carries `Exportzeitreihe: (10001027,Wasserstand,M,Min,T,1,F,Z,0,,,)` with `17,532` daily (`T,1`) rows rather than monthly (`M,1`) rows, and `fjson` reports `fileto: 0` on populated CSVs at `214536` (`Totes Weib`). Never convert `fjson` `filefrom`/`fileto` or sampled dates into `published_record_start_date`/`end_date`, and carry the `207340` daily-in-monthly-file anomaly into [#614](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/614).
- **Complete `Pegel Aktuell` telemetry witnesses (`282` river gauges):** Across all `282` telemetered river gauges, `Diagram/pegelLongterm` returns `268` `Abfluss` (`m³/s`) and `14` `Wasserstand` (`cm`) responses (`pegel_longterm_abfluss`, `pegel_longterm_wasserstand`), whereas `Diagram/pegelBgis` returns `266` `Abfluss` (`m³/s`), `14` `Wasserstand` (`cm`), and `2` `0`-byte HTTP 200 outage bodies on `201087` (`Lechaschau`) and `212167` (`Lienz`, which both also have empty `einheit: ""` in `PegelAktuell/json`). Leave `201087` and `212167` as `"unknown"` (never `"unavailable"`) on `pegel_bgis_abfluss`.
