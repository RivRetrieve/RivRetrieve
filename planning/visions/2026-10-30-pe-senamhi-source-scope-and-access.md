## Settled scope

Publish `pe_senamhi` using direct, unauthenticated JSON access from the Servicio Nacional de Meteorología e Hidrología del Perú (SENAMHI) backend (`https://servicios.senamhi.gob.pe/api`), which powers SENAMHI's [Observatorio Nacional del Tiempo, Agua y Clima del Perú](https://observatorio.senamhi.gob.pe/).

This scope supersedes the legacy WFS (`idesep.senamhi.gob.pe/geoserver/g_dbphisis/ows`) and HTML-embedded PHP chart route (`www.senamhi.gob.pe/mapas/mapa-monitoreohidro/include/mnt-grafica-new.php`) surveyed in #534 and used by `hydrodownloadR`. Fresh checks on 2026-10-30 confirmed that both the legacy PHP route and the Observatorio JSON route remain live and return the same multi-decade PHISIS daily archive (verified back to the 1970/71 hydrological year, with `previous` carrying 1969/70). Adopting `servicios.senamhi.gob.pe/api` replaces 160 KB HTML scraping with typed JSON responses, exposes hourly series and daily extrema, and unlocks SENAMHI's broader hydrological station archive.

Include published discharge and river water level across both unauthenticated hydrological subsystems on `servicios.senamhi.gob.pe/api`:

1. **PHISIS hydrological monitoring (`/api/hydrology/*`):** Discharge (`FLOW`, `m3/s`) and water level (`LEVEL`, `m` and `m.s.n.m`), covering daily published values (`type=DAILY`: `current`, `minimum`, and `maximum`) and hourly observations (`type=HOURLY`). Exclude `historicalAverage` (climatological normal), forecast products (`/api/hydrology/forecasts`), and warning bulletin summaries. Keep `previous` strictly as a potential retrieval optimization candidate for [Effort #606](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/606) only where proven lossless across leap years, never as a separate series.
2. **Surface observational station archive (`/api/station/stations/*`):** Published river water level (`m`) across SENAMHI hydrological stations (`typeId=H`), covering conventional gauges (`subTypeId=C`: synoptic `NIVEL_DEL_RIO_06H`, `10H`, `14H`, `18H` readings and `NIVEL_DEL_RIO_PROMEDIO` daily values) and automatic telemetry gauges (`subTypeId=A`: hourly `N_NIVELAGUA` readings). Exclude meteorological stations (`typeId=M`), rainfall columns (`N_LLUVIA`), and the meteorological-only historical download route (`/api/researchers/historical-download/*`).

Retrieve published source series without computing missing aggregates, splicing sub-daily readings into daily series, or merging PHISIS water-level series with station-archive water-level series unless source identity equivalence is proven. Use ordinary on-demand `LiveStages` retrieval and the shared observation cache. Do not adopt SENAMHI's separate `interoperabilidad.senamhi.gob.pe/api` key gateway, because its keys require manual institutional approval, lock access to pre-registered static public IP addresses, and publish zero `hidrologia`-group (PHISIS discharge or hydrogram) endpoints as of 2026-10-30. Exclude external portals (`ANA SNIRH`, `SO-HYBAM`) and curated catchment runoff datasets (`CAMELS-PE`, published in `mm/d` with QARTOD filtering) from runtime retrieval.

## Access conditions and operational boundaries

- **Anonymous public access:** All selected `servicios.senamhi.gob.pe/api` routes succeed without credentials, cookies, or session tokens.
- **Cloud and datacenter IP filtering:** Direct TCP connections from cloud and datacenter autonomous systems (including standard cloud CI runners) time out against `servicios.senamhi.gob.pe`, `www.senamhi.gob.pe`, and `idesep.senamhi.gob.pe`, while `observatorio.senamhi.gob.pe` responds globally. Automated CI must never depend on live SENAMHI calls, packaged offline catalogues must work without network access, and user documentation must explain this network boundary clearly.
- **Request pacing and payload behavior:** Maintain conservative request pacing (at most 1 request per second) in line with polite public-service use. Distinguish HTTP 400 validation errors (`status: false` with `validations`, such as passing `YYYY-MM-DD` instead of ISO `LocalDateTime` `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM` to `hydrogram`), successful pre-record `hydrogram` responses (HTTP 200 with 365 calendar dates whose observation fields are `null`), and successful empty monthly `readings` responses (HTTP 200 with `readings: []`).
- **Terms and attribution:** SENAMHI publishes website and Observatorio information for free public access under Law 24031 Article 4(e) and Ministerial Resolution 034-2014-MINAM, requiring explicit attribution (`Fuente: SENAMHI` on the Observatorio, alongside SENAMHI's verbatim Spanish responsibility notice on the legacy terms page). Record the legacy terms page's internal wording tension between anti-commercialization phrasing and explicit permission for use with or without profit (`con o sin fines de lucro`), keep runtime access strictly on-demand without redistributing bulk observation archives, and subject packaged offline station metadata in [Effort #604](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/604) to explicit maintainer disclosure review.
