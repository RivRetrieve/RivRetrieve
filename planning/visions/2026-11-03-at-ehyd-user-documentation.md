## Established behavior and facts

The verified provider implementation on `port/at-ehyd` (`4c1313a6eb11270bc4f299e519c1ecc2367ad345`) supplies unauthenticated live retrieval (`LiveStages`) and offline discovery over the packaged catalogue:

- **Catalogue and river scope:** `746` river station identities in `EPSG:4326`, of which `744` have selectable discharge, stage, or water temperature series across `11` products (`4,837` witnessed station-product pairs; `2` river stations, `207480` and `230102`, publish only sediment series). Standing-water lake gauges (*Seepegel*, `59` stations in `owf`), subsurface springs (*Quellen*), groundwater, hydrometeorology, and daily river sediment transport (*Schwebstoff-Tagesfracht* and *Geschiebe-Tagesfracht*) remain outside scope.
- **Separate validated and operational series:** Seven validated *Geprüfte Daten* CSV routes (`owf_abfl_tagesmittel`, `owf_abfl_monatsminima`, `owf_abfl_monatsmaxima`, `owf_wasserst_tagesmittel`, `owf_wasserst_monatsminima`, `owf_wasserst_monatsmaxima`, `owf_wassertemp_monatsmittel`) remain separate from four unchecked *Pegel Aktuell* JSON routes (`pegel_bgis_abfluss`, `pegel_bgis_wasserstand`, `pegel_longterm_abfluss`, `pegel_longterm_wasserstand`). RivRetrieve never splices unchecked telemetry onto validated yearbook records or aggregates sub-hourly readings into daily values. Static warning thresholds in `pegelBgis` and the prior-year (`last_year_value`) and 30-year climatological series in `pegelLongterm` are excluded.
- **Moving gauge-zero stage semantics and metadata:** River stage is published in `cm` relative to local gauge zero (*Pegelnullpunkt*, converted to `m` with `vertical_reference="gauge_zero"`), preserving negative values below gauge zero (down to `-2.27 m` at `207068` Linz) as well as published negative values in water-temperature exports (down to `-2.8 degC` in `owf_wassertemp_monatsmittel` at `200352` Unterhochsteg) without clamping. Because `543` of `739` historical river gauges record multiple dated *Pegelnullpunkt* heights (`m ü.A.`) across datum resets without binding historical rows to a single epoch, the `elevation_*` columns are `null` in `rr.metadata(selection)` (`state="no_metadata"` in `view="source"`) and series `vertical_datum` remains unestablished. Stage responses declaring `m ü.A` or `m ü.A.` (*Meter über Adria*) map to `m` with `vertical_reference="above_sea_level"` and `vertical_datum` unestablished. Drainage area (`orogr.Einzugsgebiet [km²]`) preserves eHYD's German decimal comma as JSON text alongside unit `km²`.
- **Archive vintage and post-yearbook gap:** Validated `owf_*` exports cover multi-decade histories up to the latest published Hydrographic Yearbook (`2023-12-31` in the `2026-10-31` snapshot and `2026-11-03` live checks). `pegel_longterm_*` covers only the current calendar year (`current_value`), and `pegel_bgis_*` covers a rolling seven-to-eight-day sub-hourly window. Intermediate post-yearbook periods (`2024` and `2025` prior to yearbook publication) are absent from federal `ehyd.gv.at` and report `unresolved` outcomes rather than `empty`.
- **Time conventions, gap markers, and `207340`:** `pegel_bgis_*` declares `MEZ` (`UTC+01:00` year-round without daylight saving), preserves `MEZ` timestamps with `time_zone="+01:00"` in `rr.fetch` (`time_zone="UTC+01:00"` in `rr.series(...)`), and converts to UTC (`time_zone="+00:00"`) via `rr.to_utc(result)`. `owf_*` and `pegel_longterm_*` omit explicit UTC offsets in responses and span historical daily-boundary shifts (`07:00` or `01:00` versus `00:00`), so `time_zone="unknown"` and `day_definition` remain unestablished. Timestamped `Lücke` lines in `owf_*` omit missing spans without fabricating daily rows and emit informational `source.gap_marker` issues (suppressing the closing-boundary `t_end; Lücke` sentinel), while interior JSON `null` entries in *Pegel Aktuell* remain `value=null` rows. At station `207340` (`Wilhering`), `W-Monatsminima` contains daily (`T,1`) minima, so `frequency` remains unestablished on that station's series.
- **Terms and checked sources:** INSPIRE Austria ISO 19139 records (`55e494dd-e0af-4806-95d6-86e30dde0504` and `6a67faa7-3ad7-4faf-91e9-17a518d10685`) license both the station network and current telemetry under `CC BY 4.0` with mandatory attribution `Datenquelle: ehyd.gv.at` linked to `https://ehyd.gv.at`. All linked publisher URLs and the practical retrieval example at gauge `209486` (`Böheimkirchen` on the `Perschling`, `2023-01-01` to `2023-01-07`) were verified against retained publisher captures and live `ehyd.gv.at` on `2026-11-03`.
