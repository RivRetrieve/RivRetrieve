# Argentina INA Alerta5 offline station catalogue

Deliver reproducible offline station discovery and source-backed metadata for Argentina's *Instituto Nacional del Agua* (`INA`) *Sistema de Información Hidrológica* (`Alerta5`) provider, `ar_ina`, under [Effort #714](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/714) and [Program #348](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/348). Verified completion of the offline catalogue completes #714; publishing this vision alone does not complete #714 or authorize observation retrieval.

Target `port/ar-ina` and keep `main` unchanged until [final review #720](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/720). Follow [provider-port step 2](../../docs/maintenance/provider-ports.md), the [evidence process](../../docs/maintenance/evidence.md), and the established [source-and-access scope](2026-11-06-ar-ina-source-scope-and-access.md). Register `ar_ina` with `CatalogueOnly()` in Step 2, matching the Swiss (`ch_foen`), Austrian (`at_ehyd`), Peruvian (`pe_senamhi`), and Australian (`au_bom`) offline-catalogue delivery precedents.

## Discovery outcome and hydrometric population

Users can discover established INA Alerta5 hydrometric gauges offline and inspect source-backed station names, water-body names, coordinates, license metadata, and recognized series candidates without network calls. Build exclusively from explicitly selected retained originals in `ar_ina-access-2026-11-06-v1` (release `436544517`) and the derived native snapshot through the shared catalogue schemas, origin gate, metadata projection, and provenance machinery.

The 2026-11-06 source audit reconciled all `4,695` rows in `GET /obs/puntual/estaciones?format=json` against all `13` in-scope observed hydrometric series inventories (`GET /obs/puntual/series?var_id={var_id}&format=json` for discharge `var_id` `40, 4, 48, 51, 52` and stage `var_id` `39, 2, 101, 85, 67, 33, 49, 50`):

- **Observational procedure and series-presence gate:** Retain only observational gauge procedures `procedimiento.id in {1, 2}` (`1` = *medición directa*, `2` = *Curva de gasto* rating curve) carrying a non-null `date_range.timestart`. Exclude simulated (`4`), forecast trace (`8`), interpolated (`3`), estimated (`5`), analytical (`6`), climatological (`7`), and derived (`9`) procedures, as well as series shells with `date_range.timestart == null` that publish no observation window.
- **Physical hydrometric network scope (`829` stations):** Exclude virtual or model nodes (`real is False` or `tipo == "V"`) and exclude the two mareographic coastal and estuary tide-gauge networks (`estaciones_shn` operated by *Servicio de Hidrografía Naval* and `estaciones_pvnymm` on the *Paraná-Río de la Plata* navigation waterway). Retain all `829` physical gauges witnessed on at least one in-scope observational series across the `9` hydrometric networks (`sat2`: `479`, `alturas_bdhi`: `235`, `alturas_prefe`: `88`, `ina_delta`: `16`, `estaciones_salto_grande`: `4`, `na-sa`: `3`, `quilmes`: `2`, `inta_delta`: `1`, `alturas_varios`: `1`), including shared *Comisión Administradora del Río Uruguay* (`CARU`) border gauge `1699` (`Nueva Palmira`, `pais="URUGUAY"`) and the `4` Greater Buenos Aires gauges with `pais=null` (`6949`, `6950`, `6951`, `6952`).
- **Inland standing-water gauges:** INA publishes no machine-readable river-versus-lake classification on `Estacion` (`tipo` distinguishes conventional hydrological gauges `"H"` from automatic gauges `"A"`). Following the `au_bom` and `pe_senamhi` precedents, retain all `829` non-virtual, non-mareographic hydrometric gauges without guessing from free-text `nombre` or `rio`; inland lake, lagoon, and reservoir gauges monitored under `Altura hidrométrica` (such as `Lago Argentino`, `Lago Viedma`, `Laguna La Picasa`, and `Laguna Mar Chiquita`) remain included and will be noted in user documentation.
- **Coordinate-less station `96` (`Colorado` on `BERMEJO`, `alturas_prefe`):** `GET /obs/puntual/estaciones` omits database rows whose PostGIS geometry is `NULL` (`GET /obs/puntual/estaciones/96` returns `404` and `?id=96` returns `[]`). However, station `96` (`nombre="Colorado"`, `rio="BERMEJO"`, `tabla="alturas_prefe"`, `tipo="H"`, `real=True`, `geom=null`) is directly witnessed in `5` in-scope stage series (`var_id` `2, 33, 39, 49, 50`) and publishes genuine historical observations. Across all `828` stations present in both `obs/puntual/estaciones` and `obs/puntual/series`, every shared `Estacion` attribute (`nombre`, `rio`, `geom`, `tabla`, `pais`, `propietario`, `cero_ign`, `altitud`, `real`, `tipo`, `has_obs`) agrees with zero discrepancies, and embedded `series.estacion` records are self-consistent across all `13` variables. Reconciling `obs/puntual/estaciones` (`828` stations with finite coordinates) with embedded `series.estacion` (`1` coordinate-less station `96` carrying `latitude=None` and `longitude=None`) preserves all `829` witnessed gauges without fabricating coordinates.

## Identity, coordinates, and station metadata

- **Primary identity:** Use the positive integer `Estacion.id` formatted as a canonical string (`station_id`, for example `"29"` or `"96"`).
- **Coordinates and documented CRS (`EPSG:4326`):** INA's OpenAPI specification (`apidocs_v3_1.yml`) defines `Estacion.geom` as a GeoJSON `Point` (`[longitude, latitude]` under IETF RFC 7946 `WGS84`), and INA's official Alerta5 web map (`https://alerta.ina.gob.ar/a5/secciones/mapa/puntual`) loads `obs/puntual/estaciones` GeoJSON directly into OpenLayers with `projection: 'EPSG:4326'` without reprojection. All `828` coordinate-bearing gauges lie within `[-72.869444, -53.731111]` longitude and `[-54.797500, -22.190278]` latitude; station `"96"` retains `latitude=None`, `longitude=None`, and `crs="EPSG:4326"`.
- **Descriptive station metadata (`station_name` and `water_body_name` only):** Expose `station_name` from `Estacion.nombre` (`829/829` populated strings) and `water_body_name` from `Estacion.rio` (`353/829` populated strings, `476` `source_null`). Preserve exact publisher scalars without trimming or rewriting, including `5` `nombre` strings and `3` `rio` strings carrying trailing whitespace or tabs (such as `'Canal de Dios\t'`) and station `"5904"`'s literal string `"null"` in `rio`.
- **Why `drainage_area` and `elevation` remain `no_metadata`:** `Estacion` exposes no drainage-area attribute, and `/obs/areal/areas` (`exutorio_id`) is INA's hydrological-model subcatchment table covering only `23` of `829` gauges (`9` with `area=0`) while mixing total basins with incremental subcatchments (`inc_*`) sharing a single `exutorio_id`. On vertical attributes, `cero_ign` (`73/829` non-null) is local staff-gauge zero relative to *Instituto Geográfico Nacional* (`IGN`) datum rather than ground elevation, while `altitud` (`10/829` non-null) declares neither unit nor vertical datum in `apidocs_v3_1.yml` and disagrees with `cero_ign` (for example station `116` carries `altitud=57` alongside `cero_ign=88.545`). Both `drainage_area` and `elevation` therefore remain unmapped (`no_metadata`).
- **License and attribution:** Bind the official *Argentina.gob.ar* terms page (`https://www.argentina.gob.ar/terminos-y-condiciones`), which licenses public national portal content verbatim under `"Creative Commons Atribución 4.0 Internacional"`. Because neither *Argentina.gob.ar* nor `ina.gob.ar/alerta` prescribes a mandatory verbatim citation string beyond CC BY 4.0 attribution, keep `provider.citation` as `None` rather than inventing citation wording, and record explicit CC BY 4.0 attribution to INA in `STATION_METADATA_NOTICE`.

## Product associations and inventory boundaries

Publish `13` canonical products (`5` discharge products in `m^3/s` normalized to `m3/s` and `8` gauge-zero stage products in `m` normalized to `m`; INA Alerta5 publishes `0` water-temperature series across `variables`):

- **Discharge (`m3/s`):** `discharge_daily_mean` (`var_id=40`, `proc_id=1`, `82` stations), `discharge_instantaneous` (`var_id=4`, `proc_id=2`, `32` stations), `discharge_monthly_mean` (`var_id=48`, `proc_id=1`, `36` stations), `discharge_monthly_min` (`var_id=51`, `proc_id=1`, `28` stations), and `discharge_monthly_max` (`var_id=52`, `proc_id=1`, `28` stations).
- **Stage (`m`, relative to local gauge zero):** `stage_daily_mean` (`var_id=39`, `proc_id=1`, `756` stations), `stage_instantaneous` (`var_id=2`, `proc_id=1`, `808` stations), `stage_hourly` (`var_id=85`, `proc_id=1`, `3` stations), `stage_4hourly` (`var_id=101`, `proc_id=1`, `391` stations), `stage_weekly_mean` (`var_id=67`, `proc_id=1`, `32` stations), `stage_monthly_mean` (`var_id=33`, `proc_id=1`, `249` stations), `stage_monthly_min` (`var_id=49`, `proc_id=1`, `83` stations), and `stage_monthly_max` (`var_id=50`, `proc_id=1`, `83` stations).
- **One-to-one witnessed series mapping (`2,611` `"available"`, `8,166` `"unknown"`, `0` `"unavailable"`):** Every witnessed `(station_id, product_id)` pair maps one-to-one (`0` collisions) to a unique INA `Serie.id` carrying verified unit `m^3/s` (`unidades.id=10`) or `m` (`unidades.id=11`). Leave `frequency`, `statistic`, `period_type`, and `period_anchor` on `products.parquet` as `"unknown"` in Step 2 so temporal support, cutoff-hour anchors (`def_hora_corte`), and datum details are established cleanly in [Step 3 (#715)](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/715). Leave `published_record_start_date` and `published_record_end_date` as `None` because `/obs/puntual/series` `date_range` is an unverified summary cache rather than certified observation bounds.
