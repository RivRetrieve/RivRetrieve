## Cover user-relevant facts and boundaries

- **Publisher, networks, and scope (`829` river gauges):** *Instituto Nacional del Agua* (`INA`), *Subgerencia de Sistemas de Información y Alerta Hidrológico* (`SIyAH`), publishes national and *Cuenca del Plata* hydrometric records through the public Alerta5 REST API (`https://alerta.ina.gob.ar/a5`) without credentials. Document the federated contributing networks (`sat2`, `alturas_bdhi`, `alturas_prefe`, `ina_delta`, `estaciones_salto_grande`, `na-sa`, `quilmes`, `inta_delta`, and binational `CARU` gauge `1699` `Nueva Palmira`), the coordinate-less river gauge `96` (`Colorado` on `BERMEJO`), excluded non-observational model/forecast procedures, virtual nodes, coastal tide-gauge tables (`estaciones_shn`, `estaciones_pvnymm`), spot calibration gaugings, groundwater depth, and empty surface-water temperature placeholders, plus the separate federal SSRH `SNIH` portal tracked in [#729](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/729).
- **Retrievable series (`13` types) and selection:** Use the shared six-column `"What you can retrieve"` table header verbatim across all `5` discharge series (`Qmd`, `Q`, `Qmm`, `Qmmin`, `Qmmax`; `m^3/s` to `m³/s`) and `8` stage series (`Hmd`, `H`, `H1h`, `Hm4h`, `Hms`, `Hmm`, `Hmmin`, `Hmmax`; `m` above local gauge zero). Explain how each published Alerta5 time series carries a station-specific numeric identifier exposed as `variant`, and show offline inspection (`rr.series`, `rr.metadata`, `rr.pick(..., variant=...)`) for selecting slotted `1 h` (`H1h`), `4 h` (`Hm4h`), or `7 d` weekly (`Hms`) stage series whose `statistic` or `frequency` is `Unknown`.
- **Stage references, station metadata, time, and data status:** Explain gauge-zero stage (`vertical_reference="gauge_zero"`, `vertical_datum` unknown, genuine negative values such as `-0.27 m` at station `8` `Andresito`), why `drainage_area_*` and `elevation_*` are `null` (`nombre` and `rio` exposed), UTC timestamps (`+00:00`), Argentina civil midnight (`03:00:00` UTC since March 2009 versus `02:00:00` UTC during pre-2009 Daylight Saving Time, leaving `day_definition` unknown), missing intervals versus stored `null` gap markers (`source.gap_marker`), and automatic multi-request splitting for long time spans.
- **Terms and checked sources:** Document *Creative Commons Atribución 4.0 Internacional* (`CC BY 4.0`) under `argentina.gob.ar/terminos-y-condiciones`, why `provenance.citation_not_established` appears as an informational issue, and required attribution to INA (`SIyAH`) and contributing networks. Verify the practical retrieval example at gauge `8` (`Andresito` on the Iguazú River, `2024-01-01` to `2024-01-04`) against both retained publisher captures and live `alerta.ina.gob.ar` on `2026-11-09`.
