# Argentina INA Alerta5 retrieval behavior

Establish and implement an evidence-backed observation acquisition and parsing contract for Argentina's *Instituto Nacional del Agua* (`INA`) *Sistema de Información Hidrológica* (`Alerta5`) provider, `ar_ina`, under [Effort #716](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/716) and [Program #348](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/348). Retrieval must acquire user-requested windows reliably across all `13` in-scope river discharge and stage products while distinguishing genuine stored observations, database nulls, synthesized grid slots, empty windows, absent series, and failed requests.

Completing the source verification, shared window-planning extension, `ar_ina` acquisition and parsing modules, and recorded/unit test suites described here completes #716; publishing this vision alone does not complete the Effort. Work targets `port/ar-ina` and keeps `main` unchanged until [final review #720](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/720). Keep `ar_ina` registered as `CatalogueOnly()` during #716; public `Operational` wiring and `fetch()` registration belong to [#717](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/717).

## Scope and boundaries

Build on [source scope and access](2026-11-06-ar-ina-source-scope-and-access.md) ([#713](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/713)), the [offline station catalogue](2026-11-06-ar-ina-offline-station-catalogue.md) ([#714](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/714)), and [series identity and physical meaning](2026-11-06-ar-ina-series-identity-and-physical-meaning.md) ([#715](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/715)):

- Cover all `13` in-scope punctual observational series routes (`5` discharge: `discharge_daily_mean`, `discharge_instantaneous`, `discharge_monthly_mean`, `discharge_monthly_min`, `discharge_monthly_max`; `8` stage: `stage_daily_mean`, `stage_instantaneous`, `stage_hourly`, `stage_4hourly`, `stage_weekly_mean`, `stage_monthly_mean`, `stage_monthly_min`, `stage_monthly_max`).
- Support both the `2,611` witnessed `"available"` station-product pairs carrying `INA.Alerta5.series_id` identities in `source_series.json` and the `8,166` `"unknown"` pairs (as well as explicit `variant` or `series_id` selectors) without inventing unestablished identities or misclassifying nonexistent series as empty windows.
- Automatic full-history range discovery remains out of scope; retrieval operates over caller-supplied `RequestedWindow` bounds.

## Empirically verified INA Alerta5 acquisition contract

Retained probes in `ar_ina-retrieval-2026-11-07-v1` (release `437200392`), alongside `ar_ina-access-2026-11-06-v1` and `ar_ina-series-2026-11-06-v1`, establish six source mechanics that govern `ar_ina` retrieval:

1. **Server-side interval containment (`row.timestart >= timestart AND row.timeend <= timeend`):**
   - For instantaneous products (`var_id` `4` and `2`, where `row.timestart == row.timeend`), `GET /obs/puntual/series/{series_id}/observaciones` applies closed endpoint comparison on both bounds (`a5-obs-52-var2-instant-point-0345` and `a5-obs-52-var2-instant-bounds-0345-0445`), including microsecond ISO-8601 UTC endpoints (`...999999Z` in `a5-obs-3346-var2-span1-20231130-20241128`).
   - For half-open `[row.timestart, row.timeend)` interval products, INA requires `row.timeend <= timeend`: a daily row spanning `[2024-01-01T03:00Z, 2024-01-02T03:00Z)` is omitted when `timeend=2024-01-01T12:00:00Z` (`a5-obs-26605-var40-daily-midday-timeend` returns `[]`) and returned only once `timeend >= 2024-01-02T03:00:00Z` (`a5-obs-26605-var40-daily-exact-0300-0300`), and a monthly row spanning `[2024-01-01T03:00Z, 2024-02-01T03:00Z)` is omitted when `timeend=2024-01-15T00:00:00Z` (`a5-obs-24674-var48-monthly-midmonth-timeend` returns `[]`) and returned once `timeend >= 2024-02-01T03:00:00Z` (`a5-obs-24674-var48-monthly-exact-0103-0203`).
   - Because RivRetrieve stamps start-anchored intervals at `time = row.timestart` and pads `RequestedWindow` by `2` days, short user windows on weekly (`7 d`) or monthly (`<= 31 d`) products and chunk boundaries on hourly, 4-hourly, and daily products require extending the rendered query `stop` by the maximum interval duration (`1 h` hourly, `4 h` 4-hourly, `25 h` daily across pre-2009 `UTC-02:00` to `UTC-03:00` DST fall-back, `7 d 1 h` weekly, `31 d 1 h` monthly) via `WindowDeclaration.stop_extension` while preserving disjoint closed microsecond partition bounds (`RenderedWindow.bounds`) in `window_planning.py` (`a5-obs-26605-var40-planned-20240101-20240105`, `a5-obs-24674-var48-planned-20240101-20240102`, `a5-obs-36030-var67-planned-20251006-20251007`).

2. **Request-span chunking (`capped-span` and `iso-instant`) without server pagination:**
   - `/obs/puntual/series/{series_id}/observaciones` returns a bare JSON array with no page-size cap or pagination cursor, even on dense 15-minute `ina_delta` telemetry (`a5-obs-3307-var2-dense15m-2d-202401`).
   - Declare `capped-span` of `365` days on sub-daily products (`var_id` `4, 2, 85, 101`, bounding dense `10`- to `15`-minute telemetry around `<= 35,040` rows per call, verified across a two-span microsecond boundary in `a5-obs-3346-var2-span1-20231130-20241128` and `a5-obs-3346-var2-span2-20241129-20241207`), `capped-span` of `3,650` days (`10` years) on daily products (`var_id` `40, 39`), and unsplit `iso-instant` windows on weekly and monthly products (`var_id` `67, 48, 51, 52, 33, 49, 50`).

3. **Upstream `skip_nulls=false` boundary-null synthesis bug versus `skip_nulls=true`:**
   - When `skip_nulls` is `false` or omitted on regular-interval series, INA's server-side `generateRegularSeries` synthesizer fabricates `id: null, valor: null` rows between the request bounds and the first/last returned database row even when genuine non-null observations exist in PostgreSQL at those exact timestamps (`a5-obs-31605-var85-boundary-skipnulls-false` fabricates `6` `id: null, valor: null` rows at `03..06Z` and `12..15Z`, whereas `a5-obs-31605-var85-wide-0100-1600-skipnulls-true` proves real stored stage values `2.14, 2.13, 2.05` and `1.13, 1.07, 1.05` exist at those hours!).
   - Passing `skip_nulls=true` (`a5-obs-31605-var85-boundary-skipnulls-true` and `a5-obs-26605-var40-gap-200608-skipnulls-true`) bypasses `generateRegularSeries` and returns exact stored `observaciones_puntual` rows (`id` positive integer). In `parse.py`, genuine stored database nulls (`id > 0, valor: null`) remain `value = None`, whereas any unstored synthesizer padding slot (`id: null, valor: null`) encountered in direct payload replay is omitted with an informational `source.synthesized_grid_null` issue rather than falsifying stored missing values.

4. **Prerequisite `/obs/puntual/series` resolution for unestablished pairs and selectors:**
   - Live witnesses `a5-obs-999999999-nonexistent-skipnulls-false` (`HTTP 404`) versus `a5-obs-999999999-nonexistent-skipnulls-true` (`HTTP 200 []`) prove that `skip_nulls=true` bypasses INA's series-existence check and returns `200 []` for nonexistent `series_id` values, while `/observaciones` rows carry `series_id` without repeating `estacion_id`.
   - Whenever `known_series` already establishes the concrete `INA.Alerta5.series_id` for `(station, product)` (and covers any explicit `variant`/`series_id` selector), `fetch.py` queries `/obs/puntual/series/{published_id}/observaciones?timestart=...&timeend=...&skip_nulls=true` directly with zero extra round-trips.
   - Whenever `known_series` does not establish the series (including any `"unknown"` station-product pair or unestablished explicit selector), `fetch.py` first queries `GET /obs/puntual/series?estacion_id={station}&var_id={var_id}&proc_id={proc_id}&format=json` (`a5-series-lookup-st8-var40-proc1`, `a5-series-lookup-st8-var85-proc1-empty`, `a5-series-lookup-st1-var48-proc1-zerocount`), verifying `estacion.id`, `var.id`, `procedimiento.id`, and `is_last_page == true` before issuing child `/observaciones` requests linked via `prerequisite_acquisition_ids`.

5. **Distinct empty, absent-series, and zero-count series outcomes:**
   - Absent series (`rows: []` from `/obs/puntual/series`, as in `a5-series-lookup-st8-var85-proc1-empty`, or a nonexistent explicit `variant`) yields `OutcomeStatus.NO_MATCH` with a `COMPLETE` empty inventory.
   - Established series with zero observations in the requested window (`a5-obs-26605-var40-prerecord-1900-empty` pre-record `1900` window, or zero-count published series `24667` in `a5-series-lookup-st1-var48-proc1-zerocount` + `a5-obs-24667-var48-planned-empty`) yields `OutcomeStatus.EMPTY`.

6. **Bounded failure isolation and honest inventory reconciliation:**
   - Transport failures on `/obs/puntual/series` or individual `/observaciones` chunks are retained via `FailedSourceRequest`, and structural payload violations (`UnsupportedSourceStructureError`, including conflicting duplicate timestamps, non-UTC timestamps, unit overrides, or station/series mismatches) produce bounded `OutcomeStatus.FAILED` outcomes with `source.unsupported_structure` issues without aborting sibling chunks, products, or stations or discarding held store coverage.
