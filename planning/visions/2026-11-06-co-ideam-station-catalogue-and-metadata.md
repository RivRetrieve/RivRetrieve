Verified completion of the offline catalogue completes #722; publishing this vision alone does not complete #722 or authorize observation retrieval.

Target `port/co-ideam` and keep `main` unchanged until [final review #728](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/728). Follow [provider-port step 2](../../docs/maintenance/provider-ports.md), the [evidence process](../../docs/maintenance/evidence.md), and the established [source-and-access scope](2026-11-06-co-ideam-source-scope-and-access.md). Build exclusively from explicitly selected retained originals in `co_ideam-access-2026-11-06-v1` through the shared catalogue schemas, origin gate, metadata projection, and provenance machinery.

## Identity, coordinates, and `CC BY-SA 4.0` station metadata

- **Primary identity:** Preserve IDEAM's exact 10-digit zero-padded `Codigo` string (`Field(NativeColumn("Codigo"))`), which keys every S3 historical CSV directly (`observaciones/historicos/csv/{etiqueta}/{Codigo}-{etiqueta}.csv`). Retain all 21 `catalogo.csv` columns as lossless UTF-8 strings (`"" -> None`) in the derived native table.
- **Coordinates and unestablished horizontal CRS (`crs = "unknown"`):** Project `Latitud` and `Longitud` through `FloatConversion()`. Because `catalogo.csv` carries no CRS header and IDEAM's secondary metadata mixes `EPSG:4326` (`spatialReference.wkid = 4326` on DHIME ArcGIS `CatalogoNacionalEstaciones/MapServer/0` and WGS84 map markers on AQUARIUS WebPortal) with `MAGNA-SIRGAS` (`EPSG:4686`) prose in Socrata `hp9r-jxuu` column descriptions, declare `"crs": Withheld()` in `STATION_CATALOGUE_ORIGINS` (`crs = "unknown"` across all `1,372` stations) paired with `WithheldFact(fact_group="unestablished_station_crs", source_id="co_ideam", facts=("source.station.crs",), reason="no_acquisition_record_established")`, matching the `br_ana` and `pe_senamhi` precedents.
- **Descriptive `station_metadata` projection (`CC BY-SA 4.0`):**
  - Project `station_name` from `Nombre` (`1,372` populated, `0` null), `water_body_name` from `Corriente` (`1,355` populated, `17` `source_null`), and generic `elevation` from `Altitud` (`1,372` populated, `unit=None`, `datum=None`, supported by `catalogo.csv` and `hp9r-jxuu`'s `"altura sobre el nivel del mar de la estación de medición"` definition without an explicit unit symbol or named vertical datum, matching `br_ana`).
  - Leave `drainage_area` as `no_metadata` across all `1,372` stations because neither `catalogo.csv` nor `hp9r-jxuu` publishes catchment areas (`Area_Hidrografica`, `Zona_Hidrografica`, and `Subzona_Hidrografica` are named hydrographic basin zones, not numeric drainage areas).
  - Bind verbatim `CC BY-SA 4.0` license (`Creative Commons Attribution | Share Alike 4.0 International`) and IDEAM attribution (`Instituto de Hidrología, Meteorología y Estudios Ambientales - IDEAM, Bogotá D.C.`) statements from retained `datos.gov.co` metadata cards `hp9r-jxuu` (station catalogue) and `57sv-p2fu` (IDEAM and third-party station observations), and carry explicit `CC BY-SA 4.0` ShareAlike attribution in `STATION_METADATA_NOTICE` and `croissant.json` following the `lt_lhmt` pattern.

## Product associations across all 20 S3 hydrological series

Map all 20 S3 hydrological `etiqueta` prefixes (`caudal_h`, `q_media_d`, `q_mx_d`, `q_mn_d`, `q_media_m`, `q_mx_m`, `q_mn_m`, `q_media_a`, `q_mx_a`, `q_mn_a`, `nivel_h`, `nv_media_d`, `nv_mx_d`, `nv_mn_d`, `nv_media_m`, `nv_mx_m`, `nv_mn_m`, `nv_media_a`, `nv_mx_a`, `nv_mn_a`) to canonical `observed_property` and units established in `catalogo/variables/csv/variables.csv` (`discharge` in `m3/s -> m3/s`, `stage` in `cm -> m`):

- **Exhaustive prefix availability (`19,690` `"available"`, `7,750` `"unavailable"`):** Every one of the 20 S3 prefix listings terminates with `IsTruncated = false` and every listed CSV has `Size >= 85` bytes (`0` zero-byte objects). Across the `1,372 x 20 = 27,440` station-product pairs, mark pairs with a non-empty `{Codigo}-{etiqueta}.csv` object in the complete prefix listing as `"available"` (`19,690` pairs) and absent pairs as `"unavailable"` (`7,750` pairs, matching `no_nve` and `usgs_nwis`), while keeping `published_record_start_date` and `published_record_end_date` `None` because S3 `ListObjectsV2` metadata carries `LastModified` and `Size` rather than observation date bounds (`Fecha_instalacion` and `Fecha_suspension` in `catalogo.csv` describe station lifecycle, not per-series record bounds).
- **Deferred Step-3 physical semantics:** Keep product-level `frequency`, `statistic`, `period_type`, `period_anchor`, and series-level `vertical_reference`, `temporal_support`, `timestamp_anchor`, `time_zone`, and `day_definition` unestablished (`"unknown"` / `EvidenceFact()`) in Step 2, handing off their source verification (including `CAUDAL_H`/`NIVEL_H` conventional versus automatic cadence, `UTC-05:00` versus `1992-1993` *Hora Gaviria*, and `nivelAprobacion` preservation) to [#723](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/723).
