# Argentina INA Alerta5 series identity and physical meaning

Deliver evidence-backed, tested source-series definitions for Argentina's *Instituto Nacional del Agua* (`INA`) *Sistema de Información Hidrológica* (`Alerta5`) provider, `ar_ina`, under [Effort #715](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/715) and [Program #348](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/348). Users and downstream retrieval code must distinguish every published INA observational series and interpret established physical and temporal facts without inheriting unsupported meanings.

Completing the research, reusable definitions, aligned catalogue metadata, and verification described here completes #715; publishing this vision alone does not complete the Effort. Work targets `port/ar-ina` and keeps `main` unchanged until [final review #720](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/720). Keep `ar_ina` registered as `CatalogueOnly()` during #715; observation acquisition, window chunking, and live store integration belong to [#716](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/716) and [#717](https://github.com/RivRetrieve/RivRetrieve/issues/717).

## Preserve published distinctions

Build on the [offline station catalogue](2026-11-06-ar-ina-offline-station-catalogue.md) across all `829` physical hydrometric gauges and `2,611` witnessed station-product associations spanning `13` observed series routes (`5` discharge, `8` stage):

- **Agency-published series identity (`INA.Alerta5.series_id`):** Every punctual series in `GET /obs/puntual/series` and `GET /obs/puntual/series/{series_id}/observaciones` carries a unique positive integer `Serie.id` (`Observacion.series_id`). Record `SourceIdentity(namespace="INA.Alerta5.series_id", published_id=str(series_id), origin=..., evidence=...)` and `variant=str(series_id)` on each witnessed `(station_id, product_id)` association (`2,611` concrete series encoded with `schema_version=2` in `source_series.json`), while preserving `native_coordinate = "var_id={var_id};proc_id={proc_id}"` on canonical `products.parquet`.
- **Observational versus simulated procedures:** Retain only observational gauge procedures `procedimiento.id in {1, 2}` (`1` = *medición directa* across `12` variables; `2` = *Curva de gasto* rating-curve discharge on `var_id=4`). Exclude simulated (`4`), interpolated (`3`), estimated (`5`), analytical (`6`), climatological (`7`), forecast trace (`8`), and derived (`9`) series, as well as undated shells (`date_range.timestart == null`). Across all `2,611` witnessed pairs in `ar_ina-access-2026-11-06-v1`, restricting to observational procedures and non-null `date_range.timestart` yields at most one series per `(station_id, product_id)` (`0` parallel-series collisions).
- **Independent network gauges (`Estacion.id`):** Co-located gauges operated by distinct federated networks (for example `sat2`, `alturas_bdhi`, and `alturas_prefe`) carry distinct `Estacion.id` station identifiers and distinct `Serie.id` series identifiers; never merge series across networks or compute aggregates across temporal resolutions.

## Physical quantities, units, negative values, and vertical reference

- **Quantities and dimensional admission:** Map the `5` river discharge variables (`var_id` `40, 4, 48, 51, 52`, `unidades = (id=10, abrev="m^3/s")`) to `quantity="discharge"` with `source_unit="m^3/s"` and `normalized_unit="m3/s"` (target `m3/s`, factor `1.0`), and map the `8` river stage variables (`var_id` `39, 2, 85, 101, 67, 33, 49, 50`, `unidades = (id=11, abrev="m")`) to `quantity="stage"` with `source_unit="m"` and `normalized_unit="m"` (target `m`, factor `1.0`). In `/observaciones` responses, `Observacion.unit_id` is `null` unless server-side unit conversion was requested; retrieval must never request unit conversion and must fail closed if a non-null `Observacion.unit_id` contradicts the series' `expected_unit_id`.
- **Gauge-zero (`cero de escala`) stage reference without datum conflation:** Retained `a5-portal-home` defines hydrometric stage relative to local staff-gauge zero (*cero de escala*), corroborated by negative river stage observations (`-0.27 m` at station `8` `Andresito` in `a5-obs-25587-var49-hmmin` and `-0.19 m` at station `52` `San Fernando` in `a5-obs-25638-var49-hmmin-st52-202208`). Establish `vertical_reference="gauge_zero"` across all `8` stage products. `Estacion.cero_ign` is populated on only `73` of `829` gauges (`8.8%`), carries no validity dates, and is never added to published stage values; keep per-series `vertical_datum` `not_established`.
- **Negative stage and zero discharge:** Preserve genuine negative gauge-zero stage readings and `0.0` discharge values without treating numeric values as missing-value sentinels.

## Temporal facts, historical Argentina DST, and source operational codes

Retained dictionaries (`a5-variables`, `a5-unidades`, `a5-procedimientos`) and bounded observation witnesses in `ar_ina-access-2026-11-06-v1` and `ar_ina-series-2026-11-06-v1` (release `436869875`) establish the temporal contract across all `13` products:

- **Explicit UTC serialization and pre-2009 Argentina DST civil-midnight intervals:** Every observation row in `/obs/puntual/series/{series_id}/observaciones` serializes `timestart` and `timeend` as explicit ISO-8601 UTC instants (`...Z`), establishing `time_zone="UTC"` and `ClippingAxis.SOURCE_TIMESTAMP` across all `13` products. Although all `2,611` series declare `def_hora_corte = 00:00:00` on daily, weekly, and monthly aggregates (which corresponds to `03:00:00.000Z` after 2009), historical witnesses across Argentina's pre-2009 Daylight Saving Time transitions (`a5-obs-26616-var40-qmd-dst-2008-spring`, `a5-obs-26616-var40-qmd-dst-2009-fall`, `a5-obs-35888-var40-qmd-bdhi-dst-2008`, and `a5-obs-24665-var48-qmm-dst-2008-2009`) prove that INA's PostgreSQL `timestamptz` store anchors civil midnight in `America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires` rather than fixed `UTC-03:00`: spring-forward `2008-10-19` spans `23 h` (`2008-10-19T03:00:00.000Z` to `2008-10-20T02:00:00.000Z`), summer intervals run `02:00:00.000Z` to `02:00:00.000Z` (`UTC-02:00`), and fall-back `2009-03-14` spans `25 h` (`2009-03-14T02:00:00.000Z` to `2009-03-15T03:00:00.000Z`). Keep `day_definition` `not_established` across all products rather than asserting a false fixed UTC offset.
- **Daily, weekly, and monthly means (`40`, `39`, `67`, `48`, `33`):** `a5-variables` declares `datatype="Average"` with `timeSupport` of `1 day` (`Qmd` `40`, `Hmd` `39`), `7 days` (`Hms` `67`), and `1 month` (`Qmm` `48`, `Hmm` `33`), stamped as half-open `[timestart, timeend)` intervals (`timestamp_anchor="start"`). Establish `frequency="daily"`, `statistic="mean"`, and `temporal_support="calendar_day"` on `40` and `39`; `frequency="monthly"`, `statistic="mean"`, and `temporal_support="calendar_month"` on `48` and `33`; and `statistic="mean"`, `temporal_support="interval"`, with `frequency` `not_established` on `67` (`a5-obs-35313-var67-hms` and `a5-obs-36030-var67-hms-nasa` confirm exact `7`-day Monday-to-Monday intervals, and `"weekly"` is outside the canonical `frequency` query vocabulary).
- **Instantaneous series (`4`, `2`):** `a5-variables` declares `datatype="Continuous"` and `0` `timeSupport`, and observation rows carry `timestart == timeend`. Establish `statistic="instantaneous"`, `temporal_support="instantaneous"`, and `timestamp_anchor="measurement_time"`, leaving `frequency` `not_established` across gauges that sample from `10 min` to once daily.
- **Hourly (`H1h` `85`) and 4-hourly (`Hm4h` `101`) slotted stage:** Despite legacy abbreviations (`Hm4h` / `altura4hora`), `a5-variables` declares `datatype="Continuous"` (not `"Average"`) with `timeSupport` of `1 hour` and `4 hours`. Overlapping captures at station `2112` (`a5-obs-6931-var2-h-st2112-202501`, `a5-obs-38398-var101-hm4h`, and `a5-obs-26438-var39-hmd-st2112-202501`) and station `52` (`a5-obs-52-var2-h-st52-202401` and `a5-obs-31605-var85-h1h`) prove empirically that `Hm4h` and `H1h` copy a single instantaneous reading onto a `[timestart, timeend)` slot (`19.23 m` at `03:00Z` on `2025-01-02`, whereas hourly `H` over `03:00Z..06:00Z` averages `14.47 m` and daily `Hmd` equals `13.90 m`) rather than computing an interval mean. Establish `timestamp_anchor="start"` on both and `frequency="hourly"` on `85`, while keeping `statistic` and `temporal_support` `not_established` on `85` and `101` (and `frequency` `not_established` on `101`).
- **Monthly minima and maxima (`51`, `52`, `49`, `50`):** `a5-variables` declares `datatype="Minimum"` / `"Maximum"` with `timeSupport` of `1 month`, and overlapping captures at station `52` in August 2022 (`a5-obs-52-var2-h-st52-202208` `min=-0.19 m, max=1.83 m` vs `a5-obs-26294-var39-hmd-st52-202208` `min=0.19 m, max=1.41 m` vs `a5-obs-25638-var49-hmmin-st52-202208` `-0.19 m` and `a5-obs-25732-var50-hmmax-st52-202208` `1.83 m`) prove they represent extrema of the underlying station observations over the calendar month, stamped with the `[YYYY-MM-01, next-month-01)` interval (`frequency="monthly"`, `statistic="min"` / `"max"`, `temporal_support="calendar_month"`, `timestamp_anchor="start"`).
- **Missing-value representation and operational vocabulary:** When `skip_nulls` is omitted or `false`, regular-interval series emit explicit JSON rows with `"valor": null` for missing intervals (`a5-obs-31605-var85-h1h-skip-nulls-false` returns `60` rows including `3` `"valor": null` rows, whereas `a5-obs-31605-var85-h1h-skip-nulls-true` returns `57` rows); zero numeric sentinel codes exist. Publisher availability codes on `Serie.date_range.data_availability` (`"H"`, `"C"`, `"RT"`, `"NRT"`, `"S"`, `"H+S"`, `"C+S"`, `"RT+S"`, `"NRT+S"`, `"N"`, documented in `a5-portal-home`) and ingestion fields (`Observacion.nombre`, `Observacion.stats`) describe operational archive state, never a harmonized quality score or row filter.
