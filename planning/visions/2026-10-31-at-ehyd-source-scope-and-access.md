## Settled scope

Publish `at_ehyd` using direct, unauthenticated `LiveStages` retrieval from Austria's federal **eHYD** platform (`https://ehyd.gv.at/services/`) and its companion **LFRZ OGC API Features / WFS 2.0** geodata service (`https://gis.lfrz.gv.at/api/geodata/i000501/`), published by the Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry, Climate and Environmental Protection, Regions and Water Management (`BMLUK`, Department I/3 *Wasserhaushalt* / Hydrographic Central Office `HZB`) in cooperation with the nine provincial hydrographic services (*Hydrographische Landesdienste*), `viadonau`, and municipal operators (`MA 31`, `MA 45`).

This scope resolves the broken legacy `MessstellenExtraData/owf` positional file-index assumptions in legacy PR #84 and `hydrodownloadR` (`AT_EHYD`). Fresh checks on 2026-10-31 established that `https://ehyd.gv.at/services/Messstellen/json?filter=owf` and `/services/Messstellen/info?hzbnr={hzbnr}` publish an explicit per-station `fjson` / `fileData` manifest mapping each deterministic `filenr` (`1..N`) to its exact filename, machine `ftyp`, and year span (`filefrom`, `fileto`), where stage (`W`) precedes discharge (`Q`) rather than following legacy `Q`-first numbering. Using `LiveStages` rather than a `BulkStore` over the 106 MB national archive (`AreaSelection/download?cat=owf&reg=10`) avoids forcing single-station callers to download and compile the full national archive and enables `at_ehyd` to serve both quality-controlled historical series and near-real-time telemetry within one provider.

Include all river-related quantities and temporal resolutions published on federal eHYD across both quality-controlled archives and operational telemetry:

1. **Validated historical river series (`Geprüfte Daten`, `/services/MessstellenExtraData/owf?id={hzbnr}&file={fileNr}`):** Full-period Windows-1252 semicolon CSVs across all nine surface-water (`owf`) river series:
   - **Discharge (`discharge`, native `m³/s`, returned `m3/s`):** Daily mean (`Q-Tagesmittel`, `owf_abfl_tagesmittel`, 628 gauges) and monthly instantaneous extrema (`Q-Monatsminima`, `owf_abfl_monatsminima`; `Q-Monatsmaxima`, `owf_abfl_monatsmaxima`, 628 gauges, published with exact occurrence timestamps).
   - **Stage (`stage`, native `cm`, returned `m`):** Daily mean (`W-Tagesmittel`, `owf_wasserst_tagesmittel`) and monthly instantaneous extrema (`W-Monatsminima`, `owf_wasserst_monatsminima`; `W-Monatsmaxima`, `owf_wasserst_monatsmaxima`) at flowing-water gauges.
   - **Water temperature (`temperature`, native `°C`, returned `degC`):** Monthly mean (`WT-Monatsmittel`, `owf_wassertemp_monatsmittel`; federal eHYD `owf` publishes no daily river water temperature).
   - **Sediment transport (native metric tonnes `t`, 1-day interval sums `Sum,T,1`):** Daily suspended sediment mass load (`Schwebstoff-Tagesfracht`, `owf_schwebstoff_tagesfracht`, 34 river gauges) and daily bedload mass transport (`Geschiebe-Tagesfracht`, `owf_geschiebe_tagesfracht`, 5 river gauges). Admission of these two series depends on a standalone shared-engine prerequisite on `main` adding the corresponding sediment mass quantities, `statistic="sum"`, and unit `t`.
2. **Near-real-time unvalidated river telemetry (`Pegel Aktuell`, `/services/PegelAktuell/json`, `/services/Diagram/pegelBgis?hzbnr={hzbnr}`, and `/services/Diagram/pegelLongterm?hzbnr={hzbnr}`):**
   - **Rolling 7-day sub-hourly telemetry (`pegelBgis`):** Unvalidated discharge (`Abfluss`, `m³/s`) or stage (`Wasserstand`, `cm`) at variable sub-hourly intervals (`5-` to `30-minute` steps) in fixed `MEZ` (`UTC+01:00`). Exclude static warning-threshold fields (`niedrigwasser`, `mittelwasser`, `erhmittelwasser`, `hochwasser1..3`).
   - **Current-year and prior-year daily telemetry (`pegelLongterm`):** Unvalidated daily discharge (`m³/s`) or stage (`cm`) from `current_value` (current calendar year) and `last_year_value` (full preceding calendar year). Exclude 30-year climatological percentile envelopes (`min_data`, `max_data`, `mit_data`, `p5_data`, `p95_data`).

Keep validated `Geprüfte Daten` series and unvalidated `Pegel Aktuell` series strictly separate as distinct source identities and selectable `variant`s. Do not calculate missing aggregates, stitch unvalidated telemetry onto validated `Jahrbuch` records, or merge sub-hourly and daily `Pegel Aktuell` routes.

### Documented upstream options excluded from `at_ehyd`

- **Lakes (`Seepegel`):** Available upstream inside `owf` (approximately 55 standing-water gauges on `Bodensee`, `Achensee`, `Attersee`, `Wörthersee`, `Neusiedler See`, and other lakes carrying `W` and `WT` without `Q`, plus the 4 live `m ü.A` water-surface elevation gauges in `Pegel Aktuell`). Defer lake gauges from `at_ehyd`'s river-focused scope while recording their availability in the research record and user documentation.
- **Subsurface springs (`qu` / *Quellen*):** Available upstream under `Sachgebiet: UWQ` / `Arbeitsgebiet: QU` (`i000501:messstellen_qu` and `/services/MessstellenExtraData/qu?id={hzbnr}&file={fileNr}`, 97 alpine/karst spring stations publishing validated daily `Quellschüttung` in `l/s`, daily `Quellwassertemperatur` in `°C`, and daily `Quellleitfähigkeit` in `µS/cm`, with no live telemetry feed). Exclude springs because they monitor subsurface groundwater emergence linked to mountain groups (`Gebirgsgruppe`) and aquifers (`Grundwasserkörper`) rather than surface rivers (`Gewässer`), while keeping them logged in the research record.
- **Non-river domains and external portals:** Exclude groundwater (`gw`), precipitation/air temperature/evaporation (`nlv`), Umweltbundesamt's chemical water-quality portal (`H2O Fachdatenbank` / `GZÜV`, gated behind a stateful Jakarta Faces wizard and a non-commercial copyright clause), `viadonau`'s authenticated partner endpoint (`HTTP 401`, whereas `viadonau`'s 34 Danube gauges are already published in federal eHYD), and heterogeneous provincial portals (`noe.gv.at`, `data.ooe.gv.at`, `hydro.tirol.gv.at`, `info.ktn.gv.at`).

## Access conditions and operational boundaries

- **Anonymous global HTTPS access:** All selected `ehyd.gv.at/services/*` and `gis.lfrz.gv.at/api/geodata/i000501/*` endpoints respond over standard HTTPS `GET` without credentials, cookies, session tokens, or cloud/datacenter IP blocking.
- **Role of LFRZ OGC API Features / WFS 2.0:** Use `i000501:messstellen_owf` alongside `Messstellen/json?filter=owf`, `Stammdaten-{id}.csv`, and `PegelAktuell/json` for authoritative WGS84 station metadata and series associations. Do not use `i000501:pegel_aktuell` for observation time series because it exposes only the single latest reading (`wert`, `zeitpunkt`) per gauge.
- **Data currency and the calendar-year gap:** Validated `Geprüfte Daten` CSVs end at the latest completed *Hydrographisches Jahrbuch* (`2023` as of 2026-10-31, followed by a trailing `Lücke` boundary marker such as `01.01.2024 00:00:00; Lücke`), while `pegelLongterm` serves only the current calendar year (`2026` in `current_value`) and full prior year (`2025` in `last_year_value`) and ignores query-year parameters. Intermediate unvalidated years (`2024`) are therefore absent from federal `ehyd.gv.at` until validated `Jahrbuch` publication.
- **Empty-body HTTP 200 responses and pacing:** Out-of-range `MessstellenExtraData` `file={n}` requests, inactive archive entries, and `pegelBgis` / `pegelLongterm` requests for non-telemetered stations return `HTTP 200` with a `0`-byte body and no `Content-Disposition` filename header rather than `HTTP 404`. Parsers and fetchers must distinguish `0`-byte responses from valid empty observation windows and transport failures, and maintain polite request pacing (at most 2 requests per second).

## Terms and attribution

Three official BMLUK / LFRZ texts govern reuse and attribution:

1. **INSPIRE and Open Data Austria (`data.gv.at`) metadata (`55e494dd-e0af-4806-95d6-86e30dde0504` and `6a67faa7-3ad7-4faf-91e9-17a518d10685`):** Explicitly licenses the hydrographic station network and current measurement services under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (`CC BY 4.0`)** for private and commercial reuse free of charge, requiring attribution in the exact form **`Datenquelle: ehyd.gv.at`** linked to `https://ehyd.gv.at`.
2. **Live `ehyd.gv.at` application notices (`main-UFXEVZGM.js`, `chunk-XKX4RDEK.js`, and `Aktuelle_Daten.pdf`):** `main-UFXEVZGM.js` notes publication under `§ 55o (2)` *Wasserrechtsgesetz* and `§ 4 (1)(2)` *Umweltinformationsgesetz*, disclaims warranty for completeness or accuracy, and restricts automated extraction except for content offered for download (*"mit Ausnahme der ausdrücklich zum Download angebotenen Inhalte"*), while `chunk-XKX4RDEK.js` (`"ungeprüfte Rohdaten!"`) and `Aktuelle_Daten.pdf` (p. 16) state raw-telemetry caveats. Both `MessstellenExtraData` per-station CSV downloads and `Pegel Aktuell` hydrograph CSV downloads (backed by `Diagram/pegelBgis` and `Diagram/pegelLongterm`) correspond directly to user-facing download actions in the portal.
3. **General eHYD user guide (`eHYD_Allgemein.pdf`, 2022, p. 14):** Contains an older own-use / no-paid-resale clause (*"für seine eigenen Zwecke zu verwenden und nicht berechtigt, die Daten auch nur auszugsweise entgeltlich an Dritte weiterzugeben"*). Direct on-demand retrieval by end users without paid data resale satisfies both `CC BY 4.0` and this clause, while packaged offline station metadata uses the `CC BY 4.0`-licensed station network with `Datenquelle: ehyd.gv.at` attribution.
