### m6-s1-undecided-shifting-offset-policy (critical)

**Location:** planning/2026-08-08-the-surface-is-three-verbs-and-a-selection/milestone-6/steps.json, node m6-s1, summary clause "the human-owned choice is between converting every row by its published offset, rejecting station/product spans containing multiple offsets, or promoting offsets to an inferred IANA zone"

**Problem:** The step leaves its own observable behaviour undecided and attributes the choice to the human, but the choice is not open: the vision and the accepted ADRs already eliminate two of the three candidates and entail the third, so the reservation is manufactured by the step graph rather than found in the inputs. A step whose behaviour is undecided cannot be planned or executed, and the step's acceptance evidence ("a mixed-offset DST-boundary regression reflecting the chosen policy") is unwritable until the policy is stated. Candidate 3 (promote a published offset to an inferred IANA zone) is forbidden by ADR-0007, which states that time_zone keeps "whichever kind the source stated rather than promoting one to the other" and that "Normalising everything to IANA requires mapping -05:00 to a zone, which several zones share, and that is a derivation ADR 0005 forbids". Candidate 2 (reject spans containing multiple offsets) would raise on rows whose zone is established, contradicting vision Scope-In item 7 ("Ship to_utc(), which raises on any row whose time_zone is unknown" - the sole refusal condition), the vision acceptance criterion "Unknown zone is refused, not guessed", ADR-0001 ("UTC is offered where the source zone is documented, and withheld where it is not") and ADR-0006's consequence ("defined exactly where the zone is known and declining where it is unknown"). Only candidate 1 survives, and it survives without residue: usgs_nwis parse already stamps each row with the offset its payload published, so a station whose offset shifts across a DST boundary has an exact per-row answer and never needs a single station-wide zone. The Program-level question the vision flags - whether an IANA identifier may be promoted from a published offset - is not on to_utc's critical path, because per-row conversion promotes nothing. The step conflated an open Program question with a decision to_utc must make, and that conflation is repairable at step-graph altitude.
