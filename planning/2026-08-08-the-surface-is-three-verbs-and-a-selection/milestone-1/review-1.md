### m1-s1-offline-regeneration-infeasible (critical)

**Location:** planning/2026-08-08-the-surface-is-three-verbs-and-a-selection/milestone-1/steps.json — node m1-s1, summary clause "Regeneration requires no network: eleven providers build canonically from committed native.parquet plus origins, while br_ana and no_nve expose offline committed-fixture generation paths."

**Problem:** The load-bearing feasibility claim is false for br_ana and no_nve. Those two providers are the only ones with no committed catalogue/native.parquet, and their offline route — generate_catalogue_from_fixture — has no committed fixture that can reproduce the packaged artifact. The only committed fixtures are tests/test_data/br_ana_metadata.json (4 stations) and tests/test_data/no_nve_metadata.json (3 stations), against packaged station_products.parquet artifacts of 52,145 and 44,001 rows. Their only full-coverage route is live: br_ana fetches https://www.ana.gov.br/hidrowebservice/... behind OAuth (_fetch_token, _fetch_all_stations), and no_nve fetches https://hydapi.nve.no/api/v1/ via requests.get and refuses without NVE_API_KEY. Executors run WITHOUT NETWORK, so an executor following the stated route either cannot produce those two artifacts at all, or produces ones that drop ~96,000 published series rows — violating the step's own "preserving every published value" acceptance and failing tests/test_package.py, which loads every one of the thirteen committed catalogues. A second, related defect in the same clause: last_catalogue_check is sourced from the caller-supplied catalogue_date (defaulting to date.today()) in br_ana, fr_hubeau (x2), jp_mlit, no_nve, pl_imgw and za_dws, so a naive re-run mutates a published column the step promises to preserve. The rename does not actually require re-running any generator — the committed parquet can be rewritten in place with the column renamed and every value, null, date type and column position untouched — but the step as scoped instructs regeneration, and as scoped it cannot merge green in an offline worktree.
