# Source annotation design experiment

[Recommendation and evidence](source-annotations/recommendation.md) for [the vision](../visions/2026-11-09-evidence-backed-source-annotation-design.md), #783. Findings return to #621 for team design discussion; no production rollout is authorized.

Recommend provider-typed linked annotation tables, a derived measurement view and explicit source slots without measurements. Three bounded genuine provider replays support the recommendation; synthetic failure/refresh controls, alternatives and limitations are distinguished in the handoff.

[Reproduction](source-annotations/integration/README.md) retains the final isolated runtime patch, a meaningful typed-column alternative and small representation controls. No shipped runtime files are changed by publishing these experiment artifacts.
